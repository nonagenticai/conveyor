{"comparable": true, "both": 0, "neither": 0, "a_only": 0, "b_only": 0,

The paired intersection — `both + neither + a_only + b_only` — is **0**: no case carries a verdict
in both arms. The verdict rule needs six same-direction discordant pairs; the instrument yields
none.

## Why the denominator is zero

- **The baseline arm refuses 5 of 12 cases on a 64 KiB source cap.** `source_context`
  (`apps/server/src/benchmark_producer.rs`, `MAX_SOURCE_BYTES = 64 * 1024`) fetches every oracle
  file whole and records `no_attempt` when one exceeds the cap. The five notes name the file:
  `options.py` (11149), `compiler.py` (11490), `related.py` (13112), `query.py` (13158),
  `fields/__init__.py` (13401). This is structural: the direct arm cannot grade more than seven of
  these twelve, whatever the model does.
- **`apply_failed` still withholds produced patches** — malformed diffs (#908): 4 of 6 in the
  direct arm, 3 of 6 in the pipeline arm.
- **`inference answered 524`** (the gateway's 100 s timeout) hit three produce jobs; one died at
  attempt 3. The pipeline arm also recorded four `the agent produced no patch`.

## Conclusion

- **#900's P1 is proven live**: zero idempotency conflicts in either arm.
- **The P3.1 bar is not met** — `paired intersection ≥ 6` fails at 0 — and the cause is not #900's
  fix. The baseline arm's source guard removes five cases by construction, `apply_failed` removes
  most produced patches (#908), and the upstream 524s remove more.
- **#12's acceptance is not met**: a decisive verdict needs six same-direction discordant pairs and
  the instrument cannot produce one at this shape. No PR.
