# Evidence — tranche-10 lesson

Proposes the tranche-10 lesson against the `retro` cadence row. The lesson is measured on tranche 9's packs: they pass the content checker while carrying only the architecture-artefact index, because the checker validates the categories a pack declares but does not require the four delivery documents or the four close-gate documents; the stamped receipt does. A closing member of tranche 10 therefore authors all nine documents before its pack is preflighted. Its evidence is the checker and the standard's class templates, which are the two halves of the gap.
