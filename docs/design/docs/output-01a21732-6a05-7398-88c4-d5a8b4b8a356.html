Benefits Diagram — P0 (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Goals and outcomes

The definition set serves the programme's strategic objectives by making each delivery project and each benefit a measurable claim rather than a wish: auditable decisions, less bespoke tooling, self-development, public collaboration, one model, predictable delivery and deliberated cross-department decisions. P0's outcome is the published definition set those claims rest on.

Measures

The seven benefit profiles carry measured baselines recorded on 2026-11-22 and re-recorded with their evidence on 2026-11-26: 0.15861 approval decisions per run, 18 tool_call rows, zero self-development runs, zero outside contributions, one model store, 28 open register findings, and 630,565 tokens per demo-credited deliverable. The measures are read from the product's own tables, not from a survey.
