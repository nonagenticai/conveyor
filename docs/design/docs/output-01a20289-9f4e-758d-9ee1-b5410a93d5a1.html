Project brief — T10 SAFe cadence and MSP tranches (retrospective, 2026-11-22)

Outcome

The conveyor has the SAFe cadence and the MSP tranche machinery. PI planning drafts cards from the backlog in computed WSJF order within the WIP slots, and only after the plan is approved; a repeated tick creates nothing; backlog refinement flags unscored and aged rows; a deliverable without typed evidence cannot demo; a retro cannot close with an untaught finding; and a benefit transition without a measured baseline cannot claim realization. Eight findings remain open; the tranche-review evidence clause is not proved in substance.

Boundary

T10 owns the cadence state machine, WSJF materialisation, backlog refinement, the demo credit, the retro lesson requirement, the tranche review and the benefit transition. It does not own the decision ladder (T2), the baseline contract itself (T1), the coach card (T4) except the retro card, metrics and metering (T11), or the one-organisation-loop acceptance (T12). The programme-wide backlog query and the programme closure path are named gaps, not part of this delivery.

Risks

  • Benefit measurement has no typed values (v1-p83-2) and ownership is a role string (v1-p83-3): a realized claim is not comparable.
  • Tranche baselines are nullable and unapproved (v1-p83-4).
  • programmes.update closes a programme without a review or realisation plan (v1-p83-5); PMP closure requires only the scope baseline (v1-p83-6).
  • The PI backlog is one project's WBS (v1-p82-5); retro lessons need no evidence and emit no kata candidates (v1-p82-6).
  • Tranche 4 is planned with no authorisation, authorised_at or baseline; T10 closed out of sequence.
