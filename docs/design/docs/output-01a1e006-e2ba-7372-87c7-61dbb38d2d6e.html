Business case — P0 (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Why now

P0's case rested on the starting state in plan §2: V1 is far implemented, the organisation spine is merged, yet the seed carries 47 projects and no programme-definition project, and T1–T17 are blocked by their SPEC_V2 §12 edges. The business case published on 2026-11-18 at 08:18:32 (01a1e006-e2ba-7372-87c7-61dbb38d2d6e, with a second document) argued that defining the programme first was the way to unblock delivery with briefs and benefit records the board could hold projects to.

Benefit map

The case is carried by six benefit profiles, of which "Benefit profile — B1 Auditable decisions" (01a1e9e0-1b2e-7353-9361-16a81a99d354, published 2026-11-18 at 08:17:51) is the representative, and by the benefits map (01a1e9e0-750d-71b9-8230-177eebe80e95) from output through capability and operational outcome to benefit and objective. The public/opt-in licensing frame is recorded as a condition of those benefits.

Cost and risk posture

The plan names the resources, boundaries, controls and cost of producing the definition set, and the delivery contract 01a1fdd4-ab0f-7802-b7a7-2541a2902e6b with approval 01a1fdd4-cac3-7767-9b0f-4c15d064d6f6 records the commitment. The risk posture is dependency-led: the typed architecture rows belong to T9 (#81), and every benefit depends on operational change after delivery. The P0 record carries no monetary risk figure.

Licensing frame

The licensing frame P0 defined is public/opt-in, and plan §3 item 5 makes it a condition of the benefits rather than a footnote. What is measurable now is that the frame is stated in the published business case and carried into the profiles. No licence revenue, take-up or negotiation appears as a P0 result.

Options, including doing nothing

P0 considered the definition options MSP requires, including doing nothing. Doing nothing leaves the 47-project seed without a programme plan, keeps T1–T19 blocked, and leaves the two partial V1 slices unexplained. The option taken was to define the programme from the existing seed rather than invent a parallel list, which plan §3 item 7 makes explicit.

Investment appraisal and cash flow

The appraisal P0 recorded is a definition-stage case, not a cash-flow model. The P0 record available on 2026-11-26 contains no monetary appraisal, discount rate or cash-flow schedule, and this retrospective does not invent one. The honest statement is that the investment case is defined in structure and unmeasured in money, and no positive return is claimed.

Capabilities the benefits depend on

The benefits depend on the architecture capability baseline P0 published — principles, tailored ADM profile, artefact standard, board charter, and the maturity and skills baselines — plus the programme-management rows and the BCM role that measures realisation. SPEC_V2 §8.3 states the rule the case rests on: a capability is not use and use is not improvement, so each benefit names both the capability and the operational area that must change practice.
