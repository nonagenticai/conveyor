Artefact standard — T9 TOGAF ADM capability (retrospective, 2026-11-22)

Common metadata

Every artefact inherits the same metadata: title, status, version, author and date, reviewer and review date, the related change initiative or project, the rationale (purpose, stakeholders, scope), and the published document or the architecture contract it sits under (SPEC_V2 §8.1). The version is incremented only by a supersession; an approved artefact's body is immutable, proved by crates/api/tests/architecture_artifacts.rs::an_approved_artefact_is_immutable.

Catalogues

A catalog names its unique identifier, item name and description of use or structure. Its entries are architecture_artifact_entry rows with one axis, so the catalogue is queryable rather than a picture. architecture_artifacts.rs::a_catalog_is_queryable_rows proves a ten-entry catalog returns its rows and that the metadata names owner, reviewer, version and document.

Matrices

A matrix names its axes, usually two. Its cells are entry rows with both axes and a value, so the matrix is joinable. The T9 artefact set uses one matrix, the Stakeholder Map, whose axes are the stakeholder and what that stakeholder needs from the capability.

Diagrams

A diagram names its building block or metamodel and its viewpoint. Its content is a document; the row carries the metadata and the viewpoint, not the pixels (plan §4.1). This is deliberate: SPEC_V2 §8.6 disclaims a live metamodel and an ArchiMate editor, so a diagram is a document with typed metadata and a viewpoint.

Links

Artefact-to-artefact edges are architecture_artifact_link rows with one of derivation, realization, reuse or dependency. The repository search answers reuse across projects, phases and plateaus, proved by architecture_artifacts.rs::reuse_is_searchable_across_plateaus. Open finding v1-p81-5 records that artefacts and links are not fully typed or projected and carry no tenant/project consistency check.

Repository

The repository is seeded from artifact_type (type, phase, plateau, mandatory flag) and filtered by the tenant's adm_profile. Artefacts are created, updated, approved, superseded and linked through the architecture.artifacts_* actions, and searched through architecture.repository_search. The standard is adopted once and grows with the assessed maturity (§8.1).
