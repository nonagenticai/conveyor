Artefact standard — T9 TOGAF ADM capability (retrospective, 2026-11-24)

Common metadata

Every artefact carries a title, status, version, author and date, reviewer and review date, related initiative, rationale, and the document or contract it sits under. In the record this is the architecture_artifact parent with artifact_kind, artifact_type_id, artifact_version, created and updated timestamps, its contract_id and document_id, and the published page it backs; the Requirements Catalog's acceptance entry requires that an artefact names owner, reviewer, version and document. The seven A–D artefacts are approved at their recorded versions, and the live Statement of Architecture Work 01a2023e-4e59-75ad-b2ab-fd7d014511d2 and delivery contract 01a20280-25ea-759d-8f96-51980017b97e are the contracts they sit under.

Catalogues

A catalogue carries a unique identifier, an item name and the description of use or structure for each entry; entries are architecture_artifact_entry rows with axis_a, axis_b, value and position, proven by crates/api/tests/architecture_artifacts.rs::a_catalog_is_queryable_rows, which returns the rows of a ten-entry catalogue. The pack's catalogues are the Driver/Goal/Objective Catalog 01a2027e-e784-7a23-9098-ca96a7610047, whose drivers D1–D8 map to their goals and objectives, the Requirements Catalog 01a2027f-1a9a-78d0-bddb-1f7d1898d3f8, whose requirements R1–R8 each name the record id or check that proves them, the Data Entity/Data Component Catalog 01a2027f-b71a-753f-adec-3d7c8185de80, which names the record entities and their invariants, and the Technology Standards Catalog 01a2027f-e9b2-7af9-9758-acc4947a9122, which fixes PostgreSQL 18 with FORCE RLS, contract states, review levels and status precedence.

Matrices

A matrix records a relationship on two axes: axis_a names one side, axis_b names the other, and the value cell carries the relationship and the engagement it requires. The Stakeholder Map 01a2027e-b3fc-7af8-a4d2-53e0ba2cca67 is the pack's Phase-A psa matrix: axis_a names the nine stakeholder positions — the sponsoring group, the Architecture Board conveyor-architecture-board, the programme architect conveyor-e2e, the programme approver conveyor-approver, the tranche-4 delivery teams, the senior responsible owner, the seed and record maintainers, tenants and downstream readers, and auditors — while axis_b names what each needs from the capability, with value influence, interest and engagement: the sponsoring group decides, the board and approver approve, the delivery teams are consulted, and tenants and auditors are informed.

Diagrams

A diagram names the building block or metamodel it draws and the viewpoint it takes, and its entries are component and edge rows rather than free drawing. The Solution Concept Diagram 01a2027f-4e4b-72dc-88de-d3ae876995c3, a diagram at Phase A on psa, draws the components that carry the capability — seed/output-standard.json, scripts/rework-content-check.py, scripts/rework-project.py, the architecture record, the /design/adm surface, the published output pages and the seven-artefact A–D set — and names the tranche-4 row 01a1e9e0-f4c0-727d-9afb-bb5b1e9783ce as the governing edge. The Business Capability Map 01a2027f-835a-7682-922b-a5ed89a1c39e, a diagram at Phase B on hld, takes the capability viewpoint and records how far each declared capability is evidenced at the snapshot, from the typed artefact repository and phase-gated lifecycle through the board, maturity and skills capabilities.

Links

Links carry four kinds — derivation, realization, reuse and dependency — and both ends must be artefacts the pack creates, or the edge is an orphan and is refused before the write. A reuse link is searchable across plateaus under crates/api/tests/architecture_artifacts.rs::reuse_is_searchable_across_plateaus, which is what lets an hld artefact cite a psa artefact instead of copying it. The pack declares one edge: the Stakeholder Map depends on the Business Capability Map. Supersession is kept as its own contract edge rather than deleted history, as the live Statement of Architecture Work 01a2023e-4e59-75ad-b2ab-fd7d014511d2 records against its predecessor 01a1f2c2-0e1a-787f-9011-46b424d1ec71.

Repository

Artefacts live in the architecture record, reached through architecture.artifacts_list, architecture.artifacts_create and architecture.artifacts_link, with entries under architecture.artifacts_entries; every table is tenant-scoped on PostgreSQL 18 with ENABLE and FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY. Publication is governed by seed/output-standard.json, and scripts/rework-content-check.py refuses a pack with a missing heading, a thin section, a template stub or an id absent from the snapshot before scripts/rework-project.py publishes it. Approval is immutable — an approved artefact cannot be edited, and a supersede writes version 2 — and the repository must not be forked into a second store: the standard is adopted once as 01a1f2dd-1e74-77e9-8b11-1ab87a0db7fa and the seven A–D rows are its record.
