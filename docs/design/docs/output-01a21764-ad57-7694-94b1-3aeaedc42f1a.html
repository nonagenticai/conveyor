Metadata

The capability map states what the platform does for the programme rather than what it runs. Four capabilities carry every delivery: build and verify, release, store and recover, and observe. Each names its consumers, so a capability with no consumer is visible and can be retired by decision.

Entries

C1 is the sanctioned compile and test path: no local Rust compilation and a bounded CI budget. C2 is the image and deployment path: the one registry and Flux's reconciliation. C3 is the data path: Postgres rows, MinIO bytes and the tested restore. C4 is the observation path: the public window and the estate's health probes.

Links

The capabilities are the vocabulary the platform's artefacts use. The requirements catalog names their verifications, the benefits diagram names their measures and the gap analysis names what is missing. No capability is claimed without a live consumer and a probe.

Document

The map is the platform's external contract with the programme. A delivery project that needs a fifth capability is asking for a platform change, recorded as a decision and a work package rather than absorbed silently into one project's plan.
