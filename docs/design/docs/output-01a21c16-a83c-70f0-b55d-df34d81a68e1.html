Project brief — S08 Governance, Modelling, Render and Export (retrospective, 2026-11-27)

Outcome

S08 delivers the programme's governance and modelling surface: portfolio registers, stakeholders, capabilities, deliverables and TOC actions over S03's spine; one flattened model shape for ArchiMate and UML; one deterministic renderer producing SVG, PNG and PDF from a single parse; an XMI exchange with stated guarantees; and two retention producers. The outcome the record closes on is the acceptance manifest at a fresh snapshot, not the built status alone.

Boundary

In scope are the 68 actions, the seven S08 tables, the two retention producers, the two byte routes and the seven Phase A–D artefacts. Out of scope are the UI (S12), model search (S07), project CRUD (S05), principle-conflict verdicts (S07), architecture containers, change-set/review/publication splits, graph layout and OCL/Profile/SysML. The boundary is the slice plan's §3, restated so a later reader can see that the exclusions are deliberate.

Reuse sources

S08 reuses S03's governed_asset, revision, catalogue_entry, trace_link, programme and project spine with RLS and outbox; S04's ActionSpec registry, invocation route and OpenAPI/MCP projection; and S07's outline/outline_node plus the principles./decisions. actions. It ships no duplicate of those surfaces: the registry dump has no S08-owned principles.* or decisions.*, and the TOC tables stay S07's.

Inception units

The build units are the slice plan's 21 steps: migrations 0008–0010; the domain and store for portfolio and models; the Part A actions and the TOC actions; the Part A round-trip; the seven model tables; the two profiles; the model, element, relationship, view and connection actions; the renderer in three formats; the XMI codec; the validation job; the four read-only tools; the retention producers; the three exchange actions and two byte routes; the served-spec lock; and the gate.

Dependencies

S08 depends on S03 (spine, RLS, outbox), S04 (registry, invocation, projection) and S07 (outline, principles/decisions). It is a tranche-5 member and its planning inputs are the PI plan, objectives, cadence and refinement named in the charter. It declares the trace/architecture surface for S12 and the model SSE topic; those are declarations, not builds.

Acceptance

The acceptance is acceptance/programme/S08.toml with 17 checks, backed by the gate binaries portfolio_roundtrip, model_profile and model_render in conveyor-api; s08_models and s08_portfolio in conveyor-store; golden and xmi_roundtrip in conveyor-render; and prune in conveyor-server. The properties are the plan's a–j: Part A round-trip, TOC legality, no double definition, golden fixtures, profile rejection, XMI round-trip, post-commit validation, id determinism, retention and artefact parity.

Capabilities

The built capability set is sixfold: portfolio governance (33 actions), TOC planning (10 actions over S07 tables), model authoring (32 actions over seven tables), validation and analysis (5 read actions), render and exchange (3 actions plus 2 byte routes) and retention (2 job kinds). The two profiles are conveyor-archimate-3.2-v1 (30 + 11) and conveyor-uml-2.5.1-v1 (10 + 9 plus 5 diagram kinds), frozen on 2026-11-08.

Plan

The plan is the slice plan's 21-step build order, completed and measured on 2026-11-08. This rework does not rebuild it: it supplies the lifecycle record the build did not leave behind, re-runs the acceptance at a fresh snapshot and closes on that receipt. The remaining work is the manual Archi 5.10 XMI round-trip and a re-read of the profile if RM's modelling tables become populated.
