PI Objectives — conveyor buildout, tranche 3

The six objectives of tranche 3, their planned business value and the measurement that decides whether each was met.

Objectives

  • O1. T13 proves the governed call path: release-grant enforcement, job storage, retirement over the zero-use report, long-op resume.
  • O2. The v1 verdict is a published table, one row per SPEC_V2 §12 slice gate.
  • O3. The three registers are operated: risks, ADRs and benefits with live rows and screens.
  • O4. The record instruments tell the truth: render, strict audit, register checks, journal.
  • O5. S04 delivers the ActionSpec derive crate and the registry and spec vocabularies.
  • O6. T3 records the change path, baseline, compliance review and closure.

Business value

O1 10, O3 9, O2 8, O4 6, O5 6, O6 5. The committed set is O1–O4 (35 of 44 planned points); O5 and O6 are stretch items for the tranche.

Measurement

  • O1: broker tests (release grant, storage round-trip, zero-use retirement, delayed-stub resume) plus the accepted deliverable's typed evidence.
  • O2: scripts/verify-slices.py exits 0 and publishes the table; the table's own negative self-test proves a failing gate is reported absent.
  • O3: live counts — fourteen risk rows, fifty-seven ADR rows, seven benefit baselines, profiles active; check-registers.py exits 0.
  • O4: generate-standards.py --check, audit-programme-conformance.py --strict, verify-findings.py and promote-record.py --check all exit 0 at the tranche head.
  • O5: the project manifest's gates execute their own checks; verify-project.py --project S04.
  • O6: T3's records exist and its manifest passes.
