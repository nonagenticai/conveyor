  • commitments: S03 commits to one domain and schema foundation every later slice builds on: the aggregate tables and their forced row-level security, the migration chain that owns them, and the typed store surface that reads and writes them. The delivery is the schema delta and its policies, not a parallel store; a later slice extends the catalogue rather than re-declaring tenancy. The contract closes on the acceptance manifest acceptance/programme/S03.toml and on the published evidence, and it does not claim the action registry (S04) or the board endpoints (S03-S06) that consume it.
