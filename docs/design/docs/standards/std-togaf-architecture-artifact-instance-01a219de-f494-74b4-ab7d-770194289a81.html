  • meeting_type is a tenant-scoped, versioned policy row: agenda_sources, required_participation, quorum, decision_rule (unanimous|majority|chair), evidence_minimum, decision_window, timebox and allowed_outcomes; a version is immutable once a meeting pins it
  • a meeting is a card owned by its chair under WIP limits and DoD, with one live instance per cadence slot (uq_meeting_live)
  • the agenda is an L0 query over agenda_sources plus explicit meetings.agenda_add actions; a deciding item names exactly one approval with its evidence
  • attendance covers job_binding and app_user seats (chair, member, presenter, observer, delegate) with department, invitation and response state; delegation only where the type allows
  • one live position per participant per item: stance (approve|approve_with_conditions|reject|abstain|request_evidence), rationale, conditions, optional commitment, surface and time; immutable once the item closes
  • close_item derives the outcome by arithmetic: meeting rule and quorum first, then the approval's policy; the stricter wins; observers never count; a conflict-excluded participant is excluded by P4; a tie defers
  • the closed outcome set is decision|commitment|action|information|deferred; commitments become project dependencies or cards and actions become cards
  • minutes are a governed_asset(kind='minutes') drafted from the typed rows and published behind the chair's gate; dissent is kept verbatim and every action becomes a card
  • a human, including the owner, holds an ordinary seat and participates live in the room or asynchronously over web, Telegram or MCP; the first decision across surfaces is recorded once and via records the surface
  • participant-hours derive from attendance and duration as the third D52 metering source; a meeting_type with no decision over a cycle is flagged for retirement
  • the /meetings console lists invitations and seats, the room with agenda, evidence, participants and live positions, the asynchronous queue, minutes and actions, and forum history and load
  • no chat, transcript store or AV product; no calendar dependency (ICS is a read of attendance); no LLM selects items, decides, infers a position or marks attendance; no second decision store
