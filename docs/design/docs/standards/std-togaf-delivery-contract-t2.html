Commitments

The record the section derives from, or an explicit absence.

Source: contract_field — 1 row(s)

  • commitments: T2 commits to one decision ladder for every gate the platform asks: the four rungs share a single stored and returned audit shape, every decision pins the policy, refinement and laya-config versions it read, and a threshold change is a tenant refinement rather than a code change. The laya rung is an inference the engine performs and verifies, not an answer its caller supplies; option sets are authored and versioned rows the answer must match; and a generated draft cannot transition a card. The delivery is the ladder family and its migrations, not a parallel decision store: later slices call ladder.evaluate rather than adding a second gate mechanism.
