The idempotency fix is live and measured

Neither arm recorded an idempotency-conflict. The 2026-11-27 pipeline arm recorded three (a request with this Idempotency-Key is still running, written as a terminal no_attempt). #900's P1 — a claim whose call's own deadline has passed is reclaimable, so the retry executes — is deployed at e6c36e664 and works.

The paired intersection — both + neither + a_only + b_only — is 0: no case carries a verdict in both arms. The verdict rule needs six same-direction discordant pairs; the instrument yields none.

Why the denominator is zero

  • The baseline arm refuses 5 of 12 cases on a 64 KiB source cap. source_context (apps/server/src/benchmark_producer.rs, MAX_SOURCE_BYTES = 64 * 1024) fetches every oracle file whole and records no_attempt when one exceeds the cap. The five notes name the file: options.py (11149), compiler.py (11490), related.py (13112), query.py (13158), fields/__init__.py (13401). This is structural: the direct arm cannot grade more than seven of these twelve, whatever the model does.
  • apply_failed still withholds produced patches — malformed diffs (#908): 4 of 6 in the direct arm, 3 of 6 in the pipeline arm.
  • inference answered 524 (the gateway's 100 s timeout) hit three produce jobs; one died at attempt 3. The pipeline arm also recorded four the agent produced no patch.

Conclusion

  • #900's P1 is proven live: zero idempotency conflicts in either arm.
  • The P3.1 bar is not met — paired intersection ≥ 6 fails at 0 — and the cause is not #900's fix. The baseline arm's source guard removes five cases by construction, apply_failed removes most produced patches (#908), and the upstream 524s remove more.
  • #12's acceptance is not met: a decisive verdict needs six same-direction discordant pairs and the instrument cannot produce one at this shape. No PR.

Artefacts

  • Run ids above are the durable handles: benchmark_runs.get, benchmark_cases.list and benchmark_runs.compare re-read them.
  • The next unit is the baseline arm's source-context cap (a whole-file 64 KiB refusal); #908 remains open for the produced-diff shape. Only after both does a 50-case verdict run have a denominator.
