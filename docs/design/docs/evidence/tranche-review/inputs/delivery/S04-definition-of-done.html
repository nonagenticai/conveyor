Definition of Done — S04 Action registry

Acceptance criteria

The acceptance/programme/S04.toml gate executes green: the ActionSpec derive crate exists and registers its actions; the registry and spec vocabularies are generated; HTTP and MCP parity holds; the served-spec lock matches; the external ingest fold round-trips.

Evidence at completion

The acceptance receipt (verify-project.py --project S04) at the deployed sha; the published project brief, evidence and closure review; the two deliverables accepted under change 01a2054b-3f86-7959-b2ad-f1edc5b0bb55; the tranche-3 demo credits on the tranche-3 cadence.

Repo gate

cargo fmt --all -- --check, cargo metadata --locked, and the repository's preflight are the compile verdict; the PR's CI and its merge are the final gate. No local compile.

Merge and closure

One issue (#3), one branch, one PR that closes it; merged only on the full suite; the closure gate decided by an actor other than its requester; the tranche review reads the receipt at the snapshot's head sha.
