User Stories — T3 PM job

Personas

  • The PM job requests and plans work: it opens the charter, plan, baseline, change and closure gates, and never decides one.
  • The approver job decides every PM gate, always as an actor distinct from the requester.
  • The programme manager owns delivery and reads the baselined plan, the change register and the closure.
  • The architecture Board approves the delivery contract the PM plans against, under the standing delegation.

Stories

  • As the PM job I can run a request end to end — charter, plan, scope, schedule and cost baselines — and read every row back.
  • As the approver I decide the charter, baseline, change and closure gates, and the record shows a distinct approver on every one.
  • As the programme manager I see a baseline change travel as a change request with an impact block and its own gate, never as a direct write.
  • As the Board I approve the delivery contract the project is closed against.
  • As the programme I close a project only against its frozen record: published outputs, an approved closure asset and gate, and the phase-G review.

Scenarios

  • Given a project with an approved charter, when the PM job calls pm.plan with WBS nodes, then the deliverable rows exist, the scope baseline is taken and its approval is open.
  • Given an open gate, when an actor who requested it tries to decide it, then the action is refused and no decision row is written.
  • Given an approved scope baseline, when a replacement is attempted without an approved change request, then the plan is refused before any WBS write.
  • Given a closure request, when the published evidence is missing, then projects.close refuses and the project stays open.
  • Given a closed project, when the record is read, then the closure review and the contract's phase-G review are published and cited.

Verify

  • acceptance/programme/T3.toml: the manifest's checks execute against the live rows (scripts/verify-project.py --project T3).
  • crates/actions/tests/spine_gates.rs: evidence-less gates refused, change impacts enforced, self-declared approvals refused.
  • crates/actions/tests/programme_baseline.rs: the taker cannot approve their own baseline; a distinct reviewer approves once; all three baseline kinds read back.
  • crates/actions/tests/closure_gate.rs: a close on missing evidence is refused.
