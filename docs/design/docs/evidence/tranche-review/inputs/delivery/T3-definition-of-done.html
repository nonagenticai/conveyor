Definition of Done — T3 PM job

Acceptance criteria

The acceptance/programme/T3.toml gate executes green: the project is closed; the scope, schedule and cost baselines are approved by a distinct approver; the charter asset exists and its gate is approved; the declared closure outputs are published; the delivery contract is live and board-approved; the Phase-G review cites the standing delegation; the closure gate was decided by an approver other than its requester; no waiver is expired; the PI cadence, plan, objectives, WSJF inputs and demo credits exist; the retro produced a lesson; no open P0 remains; the mandatory artefacts are evidenced; no P0/P1 rework package remains.

Evidence at completion

The acceptance receipt (verify-project.py --project T3) at the deployed sha; the published closure review; the change 01a2052e-5648… authorising the deliverables' acceptance; the delivery contract 01a2052f-29da-76c4-9bda-e45e0f408fe8 and its Phase-G review 01a2052f-cc4e…; the five accepted deliverables and five demo credits; the retrospective ADM artefacts (Phase A–D) built from this record.

Repo gate

cargo fmt --all -- --check, cargo metadata --locked, the repository's preflight, and the PR's CI; the served-spec lock check.

Merge and closure

Issue #74, one branch, one PR; the closure gate (projects.close_request) requested by the PM and decided by a distinct approver; projects.close runs the full record; the tranche-3 review reads the receipt at the snapshot's head sha.
