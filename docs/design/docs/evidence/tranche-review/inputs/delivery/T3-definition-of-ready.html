Definition of Ready — T3 PM job

Independent

The PM request-to-plan path ships alone: pm.plan, the four PM gates, the change path and the closure. Project creation and the board surfaces (S05) are a declared boundary, not a dependency.

Negotiable

The scope is SPEC_V2 §12 T3's three clauses; the implementation shape of each gate is the PM job's, and the manifest — not this document — is the gate.

Valuable

A governed project lifecycle: a request becomes a planned, baselined project; every gate is dual-controlled; a change travels as a change request; a project closes only on its record.

Estimable

Bounded by acceptance/programme/T3.toml's checks (project, baseline, charter, closure outputs, contract, review, closure gate, waivers, PI cadence/plan/objectives, WSJF, demo credits, lessons, product, artefact standard, rework).

Small

One project, one path, one manifest; the programme surfaces and the slice's UI are out of scope.

Testable

Every clause names its proof: the manifest checks, crates/actions/tests/spine_gates.rs, programme_baseline.rs, closure_gate.rs, pm_gate_proof.rs.

Dependencies

issue #74; tranche 3 authorised; the delivery contract and its Phase-G review (this unit); the frozen scope baseline 01a2052e-061f-7570-b780-d94101449bef, the schedule and cost baselines, and the charter gate. The B3 benefit profile's T6 dependency is recorded on the programme benefit evaluation — it is not T3's.
