Definition of Ready — S04 Action registry

Independent

Each deliverable ships alone: the ActionSpec derive crate and the registry/spec vocabularies have separate acceptance and neither waits on the other's internals.

Negotiable

The scope is the declaration path and the served spec; the screens (S12), the board (S05) and the tool platform (T13) are named out of scope, not pre-designed here.

Valuable

One declaration serves every transport: 549 actions, 580 served operations locked by hash, and a single invocation path with idempotency and audit.

Estimable

The work is bounded by the acceptance/programme/S04.toml gate checks — derive, manifest, OpenAPI, MCP, invocation, external ingest — each executable against live rows.

Small

Two deliverables under one change request: the derive crate and the registry and spec vocabularies. No third deliverable is invented to justify the slice.

Testable

Every clause names its check: the manifest's checks, crates/actions/tests/parity_http_mcp.rs, and the served-spec lock check.

Dependencies

S02 (the skeleton) and S03 (the domain schema) are delivered; tranche 3 is authorised; issue #3 is the unit. The closure depends on the tranche-3 review (this review) and the frozen baseline 01a20256-44d0-731d-b345-fffc5f3ac9e8.
