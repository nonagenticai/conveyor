PI Plan — conveyor buildout, tranche 3

The plan for tranche 3 (S04, T13, T3): the objectives this increment is judged against, the deliverables materialised for it, and the constraints the order respects.

Objectives

  • O1 (BV 10, committed). T13 proves the governed call path: release-grant enforcement at tools.call, job storage over the broker, retirement over the zero-use report, and long-op resume (v1-w4a-5).
  • O2 (BV 8, committed). The v1 verdict is a published table: every SPEC_V2 §12 slice gate is executed and recorded implemented | partial | absent with evidence (scripts/verify-slices.py).
  • O3 (BV 9, committed). The three registers are operated: fourteen programme risks with owners and review dates, fifty-seven ADR rows, and B1–B7 baselined and evaluated.
  • O4 (BV 6, committed). The record instruments tell the truth: render, strict audit, register checks and a promotion journal that replays every programme write.
  • O5 (BV 6, uncommitted). S04 delivers the ActionSpec derive crate and the registry and spec vocabularies with manifests that execute their own gates.
  • O6 (BV 5, uncommitted). T3 records the change path, the scope baseline, the compliance review and the closure with published evidence.

Plan

  • T13: the tool platform deliverable (01a20277-8d10-702f-a975-4dd6469e7b1d) is accepted with typed evidence, and the P1 batch (job storage, release grant, retirement, long-op resume) lands on the lane.
  • S04: two open deliverables (the ActionSpec derive crate; the registry and spec vocabularies) and the verdict harness that executes the manifests.
  • T3: the change path (consuming v1-change-1), the scope baseline, the compliance review and the closure.

WSJF order

Ordered by business value against the tranche's risk: O1 (10), O3 (9), O2 (8), O4 (6), O5 (6), O6 (5). The score itself is computed from the four typed inputs by the standard's wsjf_order check wherever accepted WBS rows carry them; it is never stored on the plan.

Capacity and WIP

One live-record writer; one Rust build at a time in the shared target; at most six concurrent read/audit agents; one open card per writer. The tranche is a record boundary, not a calendar: tranche 4 is authorised as soon as the tr3 review is recorded and the dependency graph allows.
