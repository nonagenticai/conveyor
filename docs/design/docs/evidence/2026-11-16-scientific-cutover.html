The tenant gate (conveyor#299)

The reference resolved the caller through licence introspection, the Authorization licence carrier (enforce mode) with an on-behalf allowlist, and refused a plain header in enforce. The reference shipped in observe; this port starts at the closure target, which its zero-caller measurement allowed (a cluster-wide sweep for the service's name, its URL variable and its port matched only its own Deployment). The port also carries no introspection cache. Both deviations are recorded in apps/scientific/src/auth.rs.
