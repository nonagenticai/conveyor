PM runs a request end to end; cannot approve its own gate; a baseline change requires a change request.

1. PM runs a request end to end — pm_lifecycle.rs

the_pm_charters_plans_and_baselines_a_request drives the whole spine through the API: pm.charter creates the project, the charter asset and the charter gate in one transaction; a distinct principal approves the gate; pm.plan writes the WBS (parents before children) and the scope baseline plus its baseline gate; that gate is approved; pm.get then returns the project, the charter asset, the three WBS nodes in order, one baseline whose approved_by is the approving principal, and both gates approved. It also reads governed_asset directly to confirm the charter gate's effect approved the asset.

Supporting: only_pm_creates_and_closes (same file) shows a non-PM job_binding in the same tenant is refused when it tries to charter or close, and writes nothing.

2. The PM cannot approve its own gate — pm_self_approval.rs

the_pm_cannot_decide_its_own_gate first proves the gate's requested_by is the PM principal, then has that same principal decide it: 409 conflict with detail the requester cannot decide their own gate; zero approval_decision rows written; the gate stays pending; the charter asset stays draft. a_distinct_approver_is_recorded is the positive control: a distinct principal's approval is recorded exactly once against that principal and the gate and asset resolve.

3. A baseline change requires a change request — pm_baseline_change.rs

  • a_new_baseline_without_a_change_request_is_refused: baselines.take on a project with an approved baseline, no change_id → 409 change_request_required, detail naming the protected baseline; the approved row is byte-identical afterwards and no second baseline is written.
  • approved_change_request_advances_the_baseline: changes.request writes an open change whose baseline_ref names the baseline it would replace and whose impact block round-trips through meta; a second principal approves its gate; the new baseline cites the change.
  • plan_without_a_change_request_is_refused: the same rule on pm.plan — no change_id → 409 change_request_required, refused before any write (WBS count unchanged, still one baseline).
  • plan_with_an_approved_change_advances: a named, approved change_id lets pm.plan advance; the new baseline row cites the approved change.
