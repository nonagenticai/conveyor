Why the denominator is two

  • No patch produced. 6 direct cases and 7 pipeline cases are no_attempt. In the pipeline arm the worker recorded the agent produced no patch for one and, for three, idempotency-conflict: a request with this Idempotency-Key is still running — a retry of agent.run colliding with the previous attempt's in-flight call and being recorded as a terminal refusal. The direct arm's six are one-call refusals (attempts=1).
  • Patch not applying. Of the six patches the direct arm produced, four failed to apply in the sandbox (apply_failed); of the pipeline's five, three did. apply_failed is withheld from the graded denominator, like no_attempt.
  • The significance rule needs six same-direction discordant pairs. At a paired intersection of 1 in 12, a 50-instance run projects to roughly four pairs — still below the bar.

What #638 did, verified live

The baseline arm no longer fails: it ran, produced patches on six cases and had them graded (4 apply_failed, 1 unresolved, 1 resolved). The grading fix is live. The remaining cap is the producer yield — production and patch applicability — not the grading path.

Conclusion

#12's acceptance — "a fresh live improvement run at a deployed revision carrying #638, with a decisive verdict (not inconclusive)" — is not met by this run, and cannot be met on the deployed revision: the verdict machinery needs six paired cases and the instrument yields one. No candidate configuration changes this, because the paired denominator is capped by the smaller arm's graded set. The producer's yield is the prerequisite, filed as #900.
