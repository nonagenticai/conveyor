Categories

The seven Architecture Skills Framework categories:

  1. General — communication, leadership, teamwork, decision-making.
  2. Business skills and methods — strategy, benefits, operating models, process.
  3. Architecture skills — the ADM, modelling, artefact production, architecture governance.
  4. Programme and project management — planning, tranches, risk, quality, delivery control.
  5. General IT knowledge — systems, data, networking, operations, security fundamentals.
  6. Technical IT skills — software engineering, languages, databases, platforms, testing.
  7. Legal environment — licensing, contracts, data protection, procurement.

Proficiency: none, aware, practitioner, expert. A rating names its evidence; an unstaffed role is none and is a gap, not a zero score to be argued away.

Evidence notes:

  • Sponsor/owner — the ADRs, the owner rulings and the public programme record are the evidence for strategy/decision-making; the business case and the licensing frame for business and legal awareness. The legal rating is awareness with advice, not authority.
  • Programme architect / maintainer sessions — the evidence is the code and its tests: the registry, the migrations, the document aggregate, the published output set, the design-pack gates and the CI. The sessions are not a legal authority and do not approve their own work (P4): the admin publish decision is the owner's.
  • Programme manager / PM — the catalogue positions exist and are not bound to a running project; their architecture and technical ratings are not assessed because no holder is bound. The PM job is T3's.
  • BCM and Board — unstaffed and not convened. The benefit profiles name the BCM as their owner while the position is unstaffed; that is a governance gap, recorded here and kata'd, not hidden.

Baseline

  • Dated: 2026-11-17, at main@4ac0ede plus the #245 branch head.
  • Scope: the roles the programme actually uses; a role with no holder is listed and rated none.
  • Method: a review judgement against evidence a reviewer can open (ADRs, code, tests, the published record); not an external assessment and not a certification.
  • Reading: the capability has a strong technical and architecture core in the maintainer sessions and the owner, and a thin programme-governance layer: the BCM, PMO lead, quality manager and the PM job are not staffed. The thin layer is why the maturity assessment scores governance low (MA-1).
