Conformance baseline v2 — accepted

Context

The U1 baseline was proposed, not accepted: 76 entries with a severity and an owner but no disposition. The corrected standard measures 22 P0 / 131 P1 / 12 P2, and the re-generated ledger carries 153 entries — 152 rework and 1 fold (S06a), each with an owner and an expiry. The programme needs the accepted version it is managed against before the rework batches start.

Decision

The delegate accepts baseline v2 at docs/evidence/conformance-baseline.json (sha256 523748a2e0785cb40aca8c821c81135eefca787cb31bc8c28fa9104cdf84d40f) under the standing delegation:

  • every entry carries {rule, subject, severity, disposition, owner, expires, reason};
  • the ledger may only shrink: a new fingerprint, a worsened severity, a missing disposition or an expired entry fails strict mode with the entry named;
  • the forward plan is re-baselined: tranches 9–13 are managed against the approved management programme baseline through one approved change request; the change and the tranche updates cite each other;
  • the 19 out-of-sequence closures and the 47 rework verdicts are the ledger's remedies, not waivers.

Consequences

  • Strict publish gating can be turned on for new records from U4; historical entries stay in the ledger until their batch removes them.
  • An entry that reaches its expiry becomes a tranche-review finding, never a silent pass.
  • The public register and the conformance page derive from the live rows and the ledger; the seed is the plan, not the record.

Evidence

  • docs/evidence/conformance-baseline.json and docs/evidence/conformance-gap-register.json at the merge SHA.
  • python3 scripts/audit-programme-conformance.py --self-test, --check-standard, and two byte-identical --snapshot docs/design/data --report runs.
  • The triage output (48 verdicts) and the exception output (19 rows), both published under P0.
