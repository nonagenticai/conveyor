Eleven capabilities map the forum's lifecycle. C1 to C3 govern, convene and prepare; C4 to C6 capture participation and decide; C7 to C8 commit and record; C9 to C11 carry human participation, metering and the console. The map distinguishes primary capabilities (policy, convening, agenda, seats, positions, decision, minutes, human participation) from supporting ones (disposition, metering, console), and it shows that the decision capability is arithmetic over the position capability rather than a summary written by a person or a model.
