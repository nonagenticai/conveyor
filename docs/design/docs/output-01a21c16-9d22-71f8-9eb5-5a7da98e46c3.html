Architecture Artefact Index — S08 (retrospective, 2026-11-27)

Metadata

The artefact set for S08 is the seven mandatory Phase A–D artefacts on the delivery track: the Stakeholder Map, Driver/Goal/Objective Catalog, Requirements Catalog and Solution Concept Diagram at phase A on the preliminary-statement plateau; the Business Capability Map at phase B on the high-level-design plateau; the Data Entity/Data Component Catalog at phase C; and the Technology Standards Catalog at phase D. All seven are authored under the 2026-11-27 retrospective rework for project 01a1d039-0d2c-7bad-8e6c-b5c5d1fd40e2, tranche 5, under standing delegation 01a1ff1f-e4ba-72dc-9c1a-13049933cf14.

Entries

The index names each artefact and its role: the stakeholder map fixes who decides and who is consulted; the driver/goal catalogue fixes the six decisions and five goals that bound the profile; the requirements catalogue carries eight executable requirements; the solution concept shows the author-publish-render-exchange path; the capability map records six built capabilities; the data catalogue records the seven S08 tables and four reused S03 tables; the technology catalogue names the two profiles, the renderer versions, the XMI scope and the vendored fonts.

Links

Each artefact links to another artefact of this pack, so the set has no orphan: the stakeholder map depends on the capability map, the driver catalogue derives the requirements catalogue, the requirements realise the solution concept, and the capability, data and technology catalogues form the dependency chain back to the stakeholder map.
