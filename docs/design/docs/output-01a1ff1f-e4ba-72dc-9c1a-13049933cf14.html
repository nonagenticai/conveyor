Standing delegation extension — the U5 programme rework (2026-11-21)

Context

On 2026-11-20 the programme owner delegated the retrospective MSP/TOGAF rework under decision 01a1fb9f-ac2d-732d-b274-1079be3dd2b3, naming conveyor-approver as delegate and conveyor-e2e as executor. That delegation covers the 48 viability verdicts, the tranche-1 authorisation, the board decisions, the management/governance/boundary programme baselines, the out-of-sequence closure exceptions and conformance baseline v2.

On 2026-11-21 the owner directed that the work continue to completion on one branch and one PR, merged only when the whole programme is correct and fully implemented, and that MSP, TOGAF and SAFe be applied with their necessary artefacts.

That instruction extends the delegated authority to the acts the rework itself requires. This output records the extension. It does not rewrite the original delegation, which stands.

Decision

conveyor-approver (01a1e8a7-8b23-76a9-9114-aafb95f7a545) is authorised, with conveyor-e2e (01a1e607-17d9-77d3-98d3-a43a87c4633d) as executor, until 2027-02-18 or the owner's revocation, to:

  • rework every project in the programme through the full lifecycle — reopen where closed, charter, Statement of Architecture Work, the A–D artefact set, the delivery contract, the acceptance manifest, the Phase-G compliance review, the project baseline and the closure — each act dated when performed and labelled retrospective;
  • decide the Architecture Board matters the rework requires: artefact approval, contract approval, and waivers only where the policy allows one;
  • authorise each next tranche from its predecessor's review, on the rework evidence at the recorded SHA;
  • create the SAFe cadence artefacts per PI: the PI cadence and refinement rows, the PI plan and objectives, the system-demo credits and the retro lessons;
  • accept the conformance baseline derived from the live record, and shrink the ledger as each batch fixes its entries;
  • measure the benefits, including token spend per accepted deliverable, and run the viability gate and the republish.

Every act cites this output's id or the original delegation's id in its evidence. A delegated act is recorded as delegated: it names the delegation, the delegate and the evidence, and never claims a human actor who did not act. No act approves its own request.

Consequences

  • The rework proceeds without waiting on a human seat, and every act is attributable to a recorded delegation.
  • The original delegation and this extension both remain in the record; the extension lapses with it.
  • A delegation of authority is not a delegation of judgement: a failing acceptance still blocks the closure it would otherwise authorise, and a red manifest is never blessed by delegation.

Evidence

  • Owner directive, 2026-11-21 (this session): finish the job; use the fewest PRs possible on the existing branch; merge only when everything is correct and fully implemented; apply MSP, TOGAF and SAFe and create the necessary artefacts.
  • The original standing delegation 01a1fb9f-ac2d-732d-b274-1079be3dd2b3.
  • Issue #526, amended 2026-11-21 to carry U5a–U5h, U6 and U7.
  • governance/standards-coverage.json — every mandatory clause of TOGAF §8.1, SAFe §8.2 and MSP §8.3 mapped to the rule, output category, row and verifier check that enforce it.
