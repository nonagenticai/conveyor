Rework mandate — every viable project is reworked by the book

Context

The conformance audit measures 22 P0 / 131 P1 / 12 P2 against the programme's own standard. The earlier draft of this exercise treated "rework vs close" as a choice; the owner rejected that: a project with a point gets its missing artefacts and its lifecycle re-run, and only a project with no live surface, no open issue and no declared-but-unpublished output may be closed by decision. The 19 closed projects in tranches 1–8 were never authorised at all.

Decision

  • 47 projects are reworked — 42 backfill-and-verify over the delivered capability, and 5 inception projects (T5, T6, T12, T15, T17) whose artefacts and contracts precede their delivery work.
  • 1 project is folded — S06a: its slice is marked superseded by S06b and says "nothing here is built or to be built"; the supersession is recorded, not deleted.
  • 0 projects are closed by decision.
  • Each rework runs the same lifecycle: charter, Statement of Architecture Work, the A–D artefact set, design gate and delivery contract, acceptance manifest and verification, Phase-G compliance review, project baseline, closure review, benefit link; each batch ends with its tranche review and authorisation.
  • A project closes only after its declared acceptance has been re-run and passed at a named SHA. A failing clause blocks the project's closure and its tranche's authorisation; a clause is never deleted to make a project pass.

Consequences

  • The public register will show reworked projects reopen and re-close through the gates; the original closure rows remain and the new closure supersedes them.
  • Pre-transition benefit baselines that can no longer be observed are recorded unavailable and re-scoped; they are never claimed as realised.
  • The burn-down ledger (153 entries, each with a disposition, owner and expiry) may only shrink; a new fingerprint or a worsened severity fails.
