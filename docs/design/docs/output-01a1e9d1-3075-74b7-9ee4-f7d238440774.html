Programme: conveyor buildout · Status: proposed for P0 acceptance (drafted 2026-11-17) · Date: 2026-11-17 · Owner: the programme architect · Reviewer: the Architecture Board (charter proposed in PF-1; rows are T9's) · Artefact: MA-1, architecture_maturity, Preliminary baseline.

Dimensions

The nine TOGAF maturity dimensions, scored 0–5: 0 none, 1 initial, 2 under development, 3 defined, 4 managed, 5 measured. Every score names its evidence; a dimension with no evidence is scored no higher than 1 and says so. The assessment is a review judgement, not an instrument reading — the evidence is what a reviewer can open today.
