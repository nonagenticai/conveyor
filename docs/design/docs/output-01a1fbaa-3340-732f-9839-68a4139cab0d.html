Out-of-sequence closures — 19 retrospective exceptions

Context

Nineteen projects were recorded closed in tranches 1–8 while every one of those tranches still reads planned and holds no authorisation. The tranche-0 review authorised tranche 1 in its decision, but the live tranche row was never updated, so the whole sequence from tranche 1 onward lacks the authorisation its projects were delivered under. The closure dates are not separately recorded; the table names the last recorded update at snapshot a33e656 and does not invent a closure date.

Decision

The delegate accepts the 19 closures as retrospective exceptions, not as conformant history:

  • the projects stay in their tranches; no row is moved and no closure is rewritten;
  • each is reworked in its tranche's batch and re-closed through the gates when its acceptance is green;
  • the tranche is authorised from the existing tranche-0 continue review as a repair, and tranches 2–13 are authorised only by their own end-of-tranche reviews;
  • the exception carries no waiver: it records what happened and the control that prevents recurrence.

Consequences

  • project_in_unauthorised_tranche remains a P0 in the register until each project's rework batch removes it by fixing the record; it is never cleared by moving rows or lowering the rule.
  • The control is the tranche gate: from U4 a project cannot close outside an authorised tranche, the close gate requires the declared closure outputs and an approved closure review, and the conformance audit runs strict at every batch.
  • The 19 are the first entries in the burn-down ledger's rework disposition; none is waived.
