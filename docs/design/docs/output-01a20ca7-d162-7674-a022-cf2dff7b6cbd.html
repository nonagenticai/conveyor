Project brief — T19 Decision forums (meetings) (retrospective, 2026-11-24)

Outcome

T19 makes the meeting the organisation's decision forum over the four existing primitives rather than a fifth unit: a meeting is a card, its agenda items resolve existing approvals, participants record typed positions, and close_item derives each item's outcome by arithmetic over one approval_decision row per qualifying seat under the stricter of the meeting type's rule and the approval's policy. After the slice the record shows who decided, for which department, with what conditions, dissent and commitments, and minutes are a chaired governed asset, without a chat system or a second decision store.

Boundary

Out of scope: any chat, transcript store or AV product; a calendar dependency (an ICS feed is a read of attendance); an LLM that selects items, decides, infers a position or marks attendance; a second decision store; a rebuild of the approval primitive beyond the participant extension; a rebuild of T9's board or T10's cadence; the T18 UI lane; and ArchiMate-style rendering of minutes. The slice must not fork the approval, card, governed-asset or dependency primitives, and it must not let a position row stand in for a decision.
