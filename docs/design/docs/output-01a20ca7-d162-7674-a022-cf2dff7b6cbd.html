T19 — Project brief

Outcome

A tenant convenes a cross-department meeting, resolves existing approvals through typed agenda items and positions, records each outcome by the forum's rule and the linked approval's stricter policy, and keeps the commitments, actions, dissent and minutes as governed rows — with any human, including the owner, deciding live in the web room or asynchronously over MCP.

Boundary

In: the five meeting tables and the two additive alterations; the meetings.* action family; the meeting card and DoD; the Meetings console destination; the governance_body and programme_cadence consumer seams; the seven decision instruments and the never-decided flag.

Out: chat, transcripts, AV integration, a calendar dependency, an LLM that selects or decides, a second decision store, a rebuild of T9's board or T10's cadence, and the T18 UI lane (#562–#566, PR #603). The approval primitive is extended for job-binding participants; no other V1 table changes.

Acceptance

The SPEC §12 T19 gate, in six clauses: a three-department majority with recorded dissent; an inquorate deferral that cannot decide; no lowering of dual control or waiving of evidence; the owner decides one item live and one over MCP, both via->>'surface'='meeting'; minutes behind the chair's gate and every action a card; a decision-free forum flagged. The plan's D1–D8 carry the per-part BDD; the implementation issue is #606.

Capabilities

Programme manager, programme architect, business analyst, project manager, the SRO/BCM and the department positions that must be represented. The organization's high-level work is the capability this brief serves; the meeting is its decision forum, not a new job.

Plan

docs/plans/T19-decision-forums.md (this unit). Implementation: #606.
