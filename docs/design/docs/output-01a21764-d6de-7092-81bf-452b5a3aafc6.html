Project Context Diagram — PLATFORM

Context

The platform sits between the conveyor programme and the services it depends on: GitHub for source and CI, Cloudflare for the public window, the tailnet for private transport, and the estate's two sites for its nodes. The programme is its consumer; the services are its suppliers.

Affected organisation

The programme owner decides the platform's charter and the tranche gates; the platform owner operates the paths; the delivery projects consume them; the Architecture Board reviews the standards. The platform's incidents reach the programme board through the register's escalations, not through an informal channel.

Affected systems

The five nodes and their roles: the control plane and Postgres primary, the registry and volumes, the compute pool, the only CI node, and MinIO with the standby. The external systems are GitHub, Cloudflare and the tailnet. A change to any of them is a platform change with a decision and a record, not a local adjustment.
