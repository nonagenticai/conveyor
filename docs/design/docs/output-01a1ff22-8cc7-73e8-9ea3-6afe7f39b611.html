PI objectives — tranche 0 (retrospective)

Objectives

Seven objectives, one per project, in outcome form:

  • P0: the programme definition set is verified and the project re-closes through the one gate.
  • PLATFORM: the standing platform row carries an approved charter and baseline.
  • DESIGN-PUBLISH: the design pack is published and its closure review recorded.
  • SPEC-V2: the specification is published and its closure record recorded.
  • S00: the usage inventory is published as evidence and the decision record closes.
  • S01: the UI-stack decision record states what is observable and marks the rest not recoverable.
  • T0: the model and contracts are published and their invariants tested.

Business value

Planned business value, typed: P0 8, SPEC-V2 8, T0 8, DESIGN-PUBLISH 5, S00 5, PLATFORM 5, S01 3. The actual value is assessed at the PI close and recorded in the tranche review.

Measurement

Each objective is judged by the project's acceptance manifest (acceptance/programme/<KEY>.toml), run by scripts/verify-project.py against a fresh snapshot at the branch SHA. A red manifest withholds the objective and blocks that project's closure. The SAFe checks in each manifest (PI cadence, WSJF inputs, demo credit) are part of that judgement.

Evidence

  • the manifests and their runs in docs/evidence/rework/<KEY>.json
  • the demo credits and the tranche-0 retro lessons
  • the tranche-0 review of 2026-11-17 and this PI's cadence rows

Risks

The six closed projects carry their old closure rows until they are reopened and re-closed; the record keeps the old row and supersedes it rather than erasing it.
