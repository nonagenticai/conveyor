Embedding

The embedding activities are the operating-practice changes: the jobs are staffed and bound, the gates become the routine the projects run under, and the benefits map is read at each tranche review. The organisation seed supplies the positions; the bindings and releases are the rows that make a position operate. The embed is not a training event; it is the programme's operating model switched on project by project.

Post-closure measurement

The realization plan names who continues measuring after the programme closes. That role is the BCM, and it is unstaffed. The interim arrangement: the programme manager records the measurements and the sponsor accepts them; when the BCM is staffed, the attribution moves. The post-closure measurement is the comparison against the six baselines, on the methods in BS-1, and it continues until the operational area's own reporting owns it.

Ownership

  • Benefit owner (BCM): unstaffed; the programme manager is interim.
  • Operational owner: the tenant whose operating area realizes the improvement (the conveyor programme itself for V2's first cycle).
  • Accountable executive (SRO/sponsor): the sponsor, who accepts the definition set and decides at each tranche review.
  • Transition date and handover: unset, because the enabling projects' tranches are not yet delivered; the plan states them as unset rather than a forecast that would read as commitment.

Gap. No benefit has a measured baseline; the BCM and the post-closure measurement role are unstaffed; no transition date is set. The plan is the method and the ownership skeleton, with every unavailable fact named.
