Standing delegation — retrospective MSP/TOGAF rework

Context

On 2026-11-20 the programme owner directed that MSP and TOGAF be applied retroactively to the whole Conveyor buildout: where a project still has a point, its missing artefacts are produced and the project is reworked from start to finish by the book; closure, waiver and retirement are not ways of avoiding the work. The operating record has no human seat to sign a decision — the owner's instruction is the delegation, and this output records it rather than inventing a human actor.

Decision

The owner delegates to the delegate identity conveyor-approver (01a1e8a7-8b23-76a9-9114-aafb95f7a545), with conveyor-e2e (01a1e607-17d9-77d3-98d3-a43a87c4633d) as executor, until 2027-02-18 or the owner's revocation, the authority to:

  • record the 48 project viability verdicts;
  • authorise tranche 1 from the existing tranche-0 continue review;
  • decide Architecture Board matters and approve the capability-level Preliminary set once;
  • take and approve the management, governance and boundary programme baselines;
  • accept the 19 out-of-sequence closures as retrospective exceptions;
  • accept conformance baseline v2 and re-baseline tranches 9–13 through change control.

Every act performed under this delegation cites this output's id in its evidence. The executor drafts the record and the evidence; the delegate approves, authorises, waives and closes. No act claims a human actor who did not act, and no act approves its own request.

Consequences

  • The authority record is repaired by dated delegated acts, not by rewriting history; the original tranche-0 review and the 19 closures remain as recorded.
  • A gate that requires a distinct approver is satisfied by two real identities (conveyor-e2e and conveyor-approver), both active members of the tenant, not by a fabricated signature.
  • When the delegation expires or is revoked, the authority to perform these acts ends with it; the records it produced remain.
