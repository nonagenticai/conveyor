Candidate Solutions — P0 (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Candidates

Two routes were considered for P0's retrospective conformance. The first reworks the project in place through the product's own lifecycle: reopen, author the missing artefacts, documents and registers, re-close through the one gate, and republish the 21 outputs from the record. The second closes the project by decision and demotes its definition set to a historical note, leaving the delivery projects to cite it without a live governing record.

Criteria

The routes were judged on three criteria: evidence traceability, meaning every claim resolves to a row, an action and a receipt; re-runnability, meaning the acceptance manifest can be executed again on a fresh snapshot and reach the same verdict; and fidelity to the programme, meaning the definition set remains the governing input the delivery projects are held to.

Options rejected

Closing by decision was rejected: P0 still has a point, because every delivery project's brief and every benefit transition depends on the definition set, and the owner's directive of 2026-11-21 requires a viable project to be reworked by the book. Authoring the missing content outside the record was rejected for the same reason: a document the engine cannot resolve is not governance evidence.
