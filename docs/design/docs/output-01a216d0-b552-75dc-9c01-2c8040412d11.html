Benefits Diagram — T10 SAFe cadence and MSP tranches

Goals and outcomes

  • Planning that is computed, not asserted. Cards materialise from the backlog in computed WSJF order within the WIP slots, only after the plan is approved, and a repeated tick creates nothing (programme_cadence.rs, cadence_jobs.rs::pi_tick_is_idempotent).
  • Demos that are earned. A deliverable without typed evidence cannot demo (demo_gate.rs).
  • Retros that teach. A retro cannot close without a lesson (retro.rs).
  • Reviews that see the record. An end-of-tranche review without baselines, benefit status or risk evidence is refused (tranche_review.rs).
  • Benefits that are measured. A transition that never measured its baseline cannot report the benefit realised (benefit_transition.rs).

Benefits and sub-benefits

The cadence is the delivery machinery the programme's benefits are reviewed against (B4/B5). Its sub-benefits: predictable PI commitments, an evidence-gated demo, lessons that accumulate, tranche reviews that cannot pass on prose, and benefit transitions that cannot precede their baseline. T10 owns no benefit profile itself; the profiles and their measurements are U6's.
