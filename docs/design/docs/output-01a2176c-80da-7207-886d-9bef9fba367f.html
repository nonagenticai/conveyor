Inspect and Adapt — PLATFORM

What went well

The platform's paths held through the rework: the hosted preflight gave a compile verdict at every pushed head, the registry served the images, the Postgres primary and its standby answered, and the record's own artifacts could be authored from the live rows. The rework's factory resumed across four blocked attempts without duplicating a single row.

What did not

Three defects surfaced only by running: the governed-asset vocabulary lagged the database CHECK by ten kinds and made the first list a 500; the plan's work packages predated the card requirement and their update was refused; and two pre-existing change rows carried no approved gate, so the WBS guard refused them. Each is now a fix with a test or a constraint behind it.

Lessons

The durable lesson is that the record's vocabulary and its database must be one list, and that a guard which reads an approval link needs the action that creates it to write that link. Both are recorded as programme lessons and one is a kata candidate; the kata is the mechanism that keeps a future session from rediscovering them.
