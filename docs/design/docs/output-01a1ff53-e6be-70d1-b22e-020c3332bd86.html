Project Brief — PLATFORM

Outcome

The platform is the standing capability every conveyor delivery project uses: the CI path and runners, the image registry and ingress, the Postgres primary with its backups and restore, and the shared workflows that make a build reproducible. Its outcome is not a closure: the platform stays live for the programme's whole life and its acceptance is the standing charter plus the evidence that each path works. This rework records that evidence.

Boundary

The platform owns the five-node estate's shared paths and nothing above them. It does not own a tenant's data model, a delivery project's plan or the programme's benefit values. It owns the cluster, the registry, the database primary and standby, the object store, the ingress and the CI runners. A request outside that list is a programme change, not a platform task.

Reuse sources

The platform's plan is docs/plans/development-process-speed.md, the 2026-11-10 audit of the Rust development process. The programme's hard rules supply the invariants: one service one database, per-service secrets, no local Rust compilation, the two-run CI budget. The estate's infrastructure record supplies the node names, the endpoints and the credentials layout.

Inception units

The platform's work packages are CI and runners, registry and ingress, Postgres with backups and restore, and the shared workflows and archetypes. Each was delivered before the delivery tranches and each owes evidence at this review: a green preflight, a registry pull, a restore receipt and a consuming workflow. The four units are the platform's standing work list.

Dependencies

The platform depends on GitHub for source, issues and the hosted preflight; on Cloudflare for the public window; on the tailnet for transport; and on the estate's power and network for both sites. Every delivery project depends on the platform, so a platform outage is a programme outage rather than one project's risk. The dependency is recorded in both directions.

Acceptance

The platform is accepted as a standing project: its charter is approved, its scope baseline is approved, its deliverable is accepted with demo credit, and its manifest's checks are green. This rework adds the artefact standard, the register and the phase-G review; the acceptance does not close the project. A later tranche review re-checks the platform with the same manifest.

Capabilities

The platform provides build and verify, release, store and recover, and observe. Each capability has a live consumer and a probe: the preflight at every head, the registry pull on every node, the restore drill, and the public window plus the estate's health probes. The capabilities are the platform's external contract with the programme.

Plan

The plan is the four work packages, the estate's five-node shape and the tranche-by-tranche confirmation that the paths still work. The platform's next steps are the restore drill, the shared-workflow consumption and the observation path's evidence. Each lands as a work package under the standing charter rather than a new project.
