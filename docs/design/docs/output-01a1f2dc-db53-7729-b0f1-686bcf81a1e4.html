Architecture principles — T9 TOGAF ADM capability (retrospective, 2026-11-22)

Principles

The principles catalog is P0's, not a T9 rewrite. Plan §2 records that P0's architecture-principles document (PF-1) was published by #122's A7 and states proposed for P0 acceptance; T9 consumes it as the Preliminary input the profile seeds. The principles the Board applies are therefore the published PF-1 set, each entry carrying its statement, rationale and implications. This rework does not invent a new principle or a new rationale; it records that the typed repository (architecture_artifact(type='principles_catalog')) and the Board mechanics are what T9 adds, and that PF-1 remains proposed until the Board adopts it through governance_body and architecture.board_decide. The catalogue's entries are rows with uid, statement, rationale and implications, so the repository can answer which artefact derives from which principle.

Approval

The Architecture Board is a governance_body(kind='architecture_board') row with membership, charter, commissioning authority and decision limits (SPEC_V2 §8.1, plan §4.2). The catalogue is approved through the Board's decision path, and the profile, artefacts and delivery contract are approved beside it. Plan §2 records the Board charter as proposed inside PF-1; this retrospective records the Board row and its charter as this capability's deliverable, not P0 content. Two ledger findings bear on the honesty of this approval: v1-p81-4, a draft Board can govern and be approved with an empty charter, and v1-w2a-3, profile adoption accepts any UUID as its board decision.

Application

A deviation is detected by the Phase-G compliance review, which records exactly one of the six levels, and is accepted only through a waiver that names the accepting owner, the reason, the additional cost and risk, and the reassessment date. crates/api/tests/architecture_compliance.rs::one_level_and_a_dated_waiver proves that a second level and a waiver without an owner or date are refused. A principle whose implications are absent is a slogan, not a principle; the PF-1 entries carry their implications and the repository returns them as rows.
