Architecture principles — T9 TOGAF ADM capability (retrospective, 2026-11-24)

Principles

P1 — Artefacts are typed rows, not documents in a library. Rationale: a document cannot be queried, gated or reused across plateaus. Implications: every artefact declares kind catalog, matrix or diagram, its phase and plateau, owner, reviewer, version and the document or contract it sits under; an approved artefact is immutable and a supersede writes version 2, proven by an_approved_artefact_is_immutable.

P2 — A phase closes on its approved mandatory set and a live contract. Rationale: a gate expressed only as prose refuses nothing. Implications: a draft artefact refuses the phase advance and names the missing artefact; a contract change re-opens the gate; a delivery project without a live contract is refused at design.

P3 — A compliance review records exactly one level. Rationale: two levels in one review cannot be compared or trended. Implications: each review records one of irrelevant, consistent, compliant, conformant, fully_conformant or non_conformant, and T9's three reviews 01a1f357-69eb-7716-811e-ce22ea93d496, 01a20284-debf-7149-ba0d-d59c876255f7 and 01a216d0-f287-70f2-aa92-af02b9ce9daa each record compliant.

P4 — A waiver names its owner and its review date. Rationale: a waiver without either is permanent. Implications: the waiver 01a1f2e0-d72c-776e-b137-a48ad1560663 carries both, and the Architecture Board accepts or rejects it without waiving a deviation it owns.

P5 — A Phase H review produces exactly one change or kata. Rationale: a review that returns an open-ended list has no stopping point. Implications: the H review closes on a single output and a second round needs its own review.

P6 — Status is read by precedence: GitHub issue state, then measured coverage, then plan frontmatter. Rationale: T9's draft plan and built measurement disagree, and a reader must not mistake the draft plan for an accepted one. Implications: the record reports the project closed and measured built while carrying the draft plan as an open evidence gap, not as a resolved status.

P7 — Every new table is tenant-scoped with ENABLE and FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY. Rationale: artefact and link visibility must be per tenant. Implications: PostgreSQL 18 is the fixed platform in the Technology Standards Catalog 01a2027f-e9b2-7af9-9758-acc4947a9122, and v1-p81-5 records that artefacts and links are not yet fully consistent.
