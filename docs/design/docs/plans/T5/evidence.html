T5 — Kata: evidence

Claim

T5 delivers the kata loop: a planned procedure change opens a kata whose prediction is recorded before its measurement, the measurement runs on a held-out split, a measured regression reverts the candidate, a flat or incomparable result adopts nothing, the kata cannot approve its own adoption, and the open/adopt gates refuse the documented cases.
