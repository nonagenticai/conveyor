Project brief — proof of delivery

  • Programme: conveyor buildout · tranche 1 (authorised)
  • Plan: docs/plans/proof-of-delivery.md
  • Units: #744 (capability), #746 (corpus, certificates, live gate)
  • ADM: phase G, capability plateau

Outcome

A delivered slice or work package can be handed to a third party with a certificate that says what was claimed, which facts it was checked against, and the exact Lean source that closed the claim — and that party can re-run the check without trusting the conveyor.

Why now

The Lean prover is already a workspace crate and a declared set of external actions (crates/actions/src/integrations/lean4.rs). Gate evidence is already a closed, typed set (resolve_evidence). The only missing pieces are the durable certificate and one evidence kind, so the marginal cost is small and the evidence model stops being prose.

Scope

In: the proof store, proofs.* actions, the lean_proof evidence kind, the Lean model and facts renderer, programme certificates, package-manifest proofs, and the live gate on the programme release.

Out: Rust-level verification, model-to-implementation refinement, prover-side attestation, and widening the requirement to every card's Definition of Done before one real cycle of refusals.

Benefits

Enabling for B1 — Auditable decisions (the certificate is the evidence a decision row cites) and for B6 — Predictable delivery (the release gate reads a bundle rather than a checklist). No new benefit instrument is introduced.

Risks

  • The prover allows two active lanes shared with other tenants; proof work is capped at one concurrent call and honours Retry-After.
  • Kernel reduction over large literals can exceed the 120-second cap; the measurement step records the real numbers before anything depends on them, and native_decide is recorded as a different trust claim when used.
