Change control

A change to scope, baseline, the census or the architecture goes through the change-control template: the request, its impact (scope/schedule/cost/benefit/risk), the affected contract and the decision. A re-baseline is a signed commit stating what changed. A deviation from the architecture (a second backend, a fixture, a canvas-only work surface, a seat that grants authority) is a change request; a silent fork is refused. A change after the SoAW approval is a new contract version and re-opens the gate.

Closure review

The project closes only on the P6 gate, not on "the UI looks finished": the instrument green with no open exemption; every destination verified; the named flows round-trip; a11y and performance met; the owner's primacy decision recorded (or the walk recorded as an alternative client); the delivery contract's compliance review at one level; the benefit profile's post-measurement recorded or unmeasured. A closed project updates the slice's coverage row and the programme register; a retirement of apps/web is a separate, later decision.
