GEMBA-WALK — Statement of intent

Intended use

The Gemba Walk is a secondary, game-style client of the conveyor, served at https://game.nonagentic.ai/ with its own conveyor-game Keycloak client. It reads and writes the same records as the operator console; it is not a second backend or a decision store.

Ownership

The GEMBA-WALK programme project owns the walk. apps/web remains normative.

Measures of effectiveness

  • 56/56 census routes reachable.
  • The P6 gate green.
  • The soak within 1.5× the warm baseline (measured 1.059× over 702 visits).
  • The accessibility budgets met.
  • Sign-in through conveyor-game, proving the live path.
