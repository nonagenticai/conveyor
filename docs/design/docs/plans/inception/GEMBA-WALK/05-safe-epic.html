GEMBA-WALK — SAFe epic

Amendment 2026-11-27 (CR-WALK-001, ADR 0024). The client is served at https://game.nonagentic.ai/ with its own public client conveyor-game; F1, the enablers and PI-4 are updated, and the identity is owned delivery work rather than an external redirect.

Epic

Hypothesis. If the conveyor serves a second, contract-faithful client at https://game.nonagentic.ai/ whose every read and write goes through the existing registry, then the programme can measure whether the action contract is UI-independent, and the owner can decide primacy on a frozen census and per-destination evidence instead of a mock.

Outcome. The P6 gate: every §10.5 destination and retained V1 route has a walk route and a Playwright spec asserting empty, error, happy and one refusal or write; every action in the frozen census is reachable and schema-rendered; the named write flows round-trip with the correct headers; the accessibility and performance budgets are met; the owner signs the primacy decision — or the walk remains an alternative client.

Non-goals. A second backend, decision store or read model; SSR; native mobile; chat, audio or video; customer care; a replacement for /design/; a public unauthenticated mode; pixel parity with the mock; retiring apps/web before the gate; any new table, action, event or port.

Demo evidence

The system demo exhibits: the census reproducing the baseline; the instrument failing on an unreachable action and passing after the fix; the spike's PKCE redirect, SSE update, a read form, a write with If-Match, a destructive action refused without approval, and a deep link; the game host's entry and boundary, the real conveyor-game sign-in, and the /walk 308; the foundation's Portfolio read and one real write; one read and one write per wing; the retained V1 routes; the a11y and performance measurements; the P6 decision. No demo, no credit.

Inspect & adapt

The retro reads the five scripted decision-task times in both clients, the census's absent/ unreachable counts, the spec pass rate, the accessibility findings and the performance budgets. A systemic contract finding becomes a change request on both clients; a UX finding becomes a kata candidate (T5), not a walk-only patch. A destination that cannot reach parity without backend surface is a stop-and-escalate, never a backend addition.
