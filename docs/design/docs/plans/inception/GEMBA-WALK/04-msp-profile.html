GEMBA-WALK — MSP profile

1. Business case addendum

Why now. The conveyor has one operator UI, a Leptos console, and one local mock of a different interaction model. The owner is considering the walk as the primary UI, but the mock has no auth, no registry and no live rows: it cannot be measured, and it cannot be promoted. The programme also cannot answer a basic architectural question — is the action contract genuinely UI-independent? — by assertion. A second client built on the same contract answers it, and the parity instrument makes the answer falsifiable.

Options.

Option What it buys What it costs
Do nothing — the mock stays local a design reference no evidence, no live client, the primacy question stays open
Promote the mock speed no auth, no registry, fixtures — the coverage record would lie; rejected by ADR 0013/D64
A game mode inside apps/web one bundle a frame loop (and possibly a WASM engine) in the operator bundle; one release cadence for two surfaces; the engine spike unmeasurable
A separate client at /walk/ (GEMBA-WALK) a real, measurable second client; a reversible primacy path a second shell, auth flow and browser suite; the parity instrument and specs are the control

Cost and effort. One static sub-site, one router registry, one sheet/action-sheet system, one world port and canvas baseline, one Playwright suite and one instrument. The work is staged P0–P6 and split across five build issues; no new backend, table, action or port. The recurring cost is the maintenance of two clients, owned by the same contract and the same registry.

Investment appraisal. The benefit is measured by the decision-task instrument in §2, not by the capability's existence. The case is accepted provisionally: the baseline is unmeasured until the P0 spike measures the same scripted task in apps/web; the P6 review decides whether the instrument shows a real improvement. An unmeasured baseline is reported unmeasured, never zero. No token-spend KPI is used (owner decision 2026-11-25).

6. Tranche authorisation

GEMBA-WALK is a cross-cutting programme unit under tranche 0 (the CROSS_CUTTING convention used by P0, PLATFORM and DESIGN-PUBLISH), not a new tranche. Its entry is the accepted ADR 0021 and the published inception set; its exit is the P6 parity gate and the closure review, not a tranche review. The P6 decision is the owner's primacy decision; a re-baseline of the census is a signed commit that states what changed, and a scope change is a change request (§7).

7. Transition and measurement

The old practice is apps/web only; the baseline is measured while it is still the only live client (the P0 spike times the five named decision flows in apps/web, and records the census). GEMBA-WALK enacts the new practice: the walk reaches parity, the seats use it for the named flows, and the same scripted tasks are timed in both clients. Post-transition the BCM measures the five flows and the census, reports the delta against the apps/web baseline, and keeps measuring until the realization plan names who continues after programme closure. The BCM is the benefit owner; the programme manager is accountable for the instrument's operation. Primacy does not retire apps/web: retirement is a separate decision after a stabilisation period.
