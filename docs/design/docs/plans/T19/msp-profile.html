T19 — MSP profile

1. Business case addendum

Why now. V2's high-level work — programme management, architecture, analysis and project management — is decided in cross-department meetings, but only the resulting approval row survives. The programme cannot show the trade-offs, conditions, dissent or commitments that made the decision, and it cannot tell whether the forum that was supposed to decide actually met.

Options.

Option What it buys What it costs
Do nothing — approvals only the decision row no deliberation, no cross-department positions, no dissent, no commitments; R20 unmitigated
A chat/transcript room conversation no typed decision, no quorum, no arithmetic, a second store; rejected by §6.8
Typed meeting forum (T19) decision + deliberation, one decision store, measurable forums five tables, twelve actions, one console destination, one participant extension

Cost and effort. One delivery slice (D1–D8), one migration set, the meetings.* family and the Meetings destination. The ongoing cost is participant time, metered as participant-hours from attendance × duration in the D52 roll-up. The change is a tenant policy, not a new runtime.

Investment appraisal. The benefit is measured by the instruments in §3, not by the capability's existence. The case is accepted provisionally: the baseline is unmeasured until the first meeting rows exist, and the tranche-4 review decides whether the instrument is producing a measurable improvement. An unmeasured baseline is reported unmeasured, never zero.

6. Tranche authorisation

T19 is authorised under tranche 4. Its entry is the tranche-3 exit (T1, T2, T3 and P0 built); its exit is the §12 T19 gate with the D1–D8 evidence. The tranche review's evidence is the baseline for B7 (unmeasured at entry), the R20 posture, the meeting instruments from D7 and the blueprint alignment from the architecture design. The review decides continue, pause or stop; a re-baseline goes through change control, never through the review.

7. Transition and measurement

The old practice is approval-only; the baseline is measured while it is still observable (zero meeting rows, decision latency recorded only on the approval). T19 enacts the new practice: convene the forum, record positions, derive outcomes, publish minutes. Post-transition the BCM measures the seven instruments, reports the delta against the approval-only baseline and keeps measuring until the realization plan names who continues after programme closure. The BCM is the benefit owner; the programme manager is accountable for the forums' operation.
