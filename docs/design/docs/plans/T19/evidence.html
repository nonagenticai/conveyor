T19 — Decision forums: evidence

Claim

Issue #606's D1–D8 are implemented on issue/606-t19-meetings: the meeting is a typed card whose agenda resolves existing approvals, whose seats record positions, and whose outcome is derived by arithmetic over those positions and the linked approval's stricter policy — with the human seat available in the web room, over Telegram and over MCP, and the minutes a chaired governed asset.

Gap

The deployment-gated live proof — the owner deciding one item in the web room and one over MCP at the deployed revision, both via='meeting' — is acceptance-blocked by the lane release, not waived: the unit tests prove the two surfaces and the first-decision rule, and the Playwright spec proves the room. No other gap is claimed; the type-authoring route (a policy change is a new meeting_type version through the governance route) is a planned follow-up, not part of D1–D8.
