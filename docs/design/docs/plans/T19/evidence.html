T19 — Decision forums: evidence

Claim

Issue #606's D1–D8 are implemented on issue/606-t19-meetings and exercised on the deployed engine (a8f67c3e): the meeting is a typed card whose agenda resolves existing approvals, whose seats record positions, and whose outcome is derived by arithmetic over those positions and the linked approval's stricter policy — with the human seat available in the web room, over Telegram and over MCP, and the minutes a chaired governed asset. The live gate is recorded below.
