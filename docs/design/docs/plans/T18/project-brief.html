Project brief — T18, the real V2 UI

Outcome

A signed-in tenant runs every V2 destination from the conveyor's own application, and the programme measures those screens from the app's real routes and specs instead of the design mock.

Boundary

In scope: the application shell and navigation, the ten V2 destinations in SPEC_V2 §10.5, the L1–L4 delivery view, one Playwright spec per destination, and the measurement of the real routes. Out of scope: new tables, actions, events or ports; SSR, a mobile app or a second router; retiring the design pack or rebuilding the public programme site; any live tenant write inside a pull request. The V1 routes stay; the pack stays the public site at /design/ and the reviewed design reference (SPEC_V2 D64).

Reuse sources

  • apps/web/src/app.rs — the router, sidebar and top bar; api.rs, auth.rs, state.rs, components.rs, sse.rs, palette.rs.
  • apps/web/e2e/fixture.ts, apps/web/dev-server.mjs, apps/web/playwright.config.ts and the existing 31 specs.
  • The compiled action registry (SPEC_V2 §10.4) and the existing V1 screens: boards, cockpit, processes, process map, evals, usage, prompt audit, platform, settings.
  • docs/design/v2.js and docs/design/STUDIO-SPEC.md as the reviewed design reference, never as production code.
  • The delivery view's derived sections (programme, work, measure) published by the pack and exposed by the owning slices' read actions.

Inception units

  • docs/plans/T18/inception-analysis.md — what exists in the app, what is reused, and the risks.
  • docs/plans/T18/architecture-design.md — the route/read/write contracts, the decisions and the architecture artefacts.

Dependencies

  • blocked-by (construction): T0, T1, T2, T3, P0 — the model and contracts, the plan spine, the decision ladder, the PM job and the programme definition.
  • acceptance-blocked-by: T4, T5, T7, T9, T10, T11, T13, T16, T17 — each destination's live scenarios wait on its owning slice; construction proceeds with the honest "not yet live" state.
  • acceptance-blocked-by: T12 — the final organisation loop is T12's acceptance driven from the UI.

Acceptance

  • The SPEC_V2 §12 T18 gate: every destination route exists with its spec; the signed-in tenant drives the portfolio, programme, plan, organisation, procedures, tools, improve and operations paths; the coverage record reports exists only from a real route and its spec.
  • Each destination's Playwright spec asserts empty, error, happy and one refusal; web.yml is the browser gate.

Capabilities

The tenant operator who runs the portfolio, programme, plan, organisation, procedures, authoring, tools, improve and operations surfaces; and the PM, kanban-coach, kata, development and operations jobs those destinations serve. The UI is their surface, not a new job.

Plan

docs/plans/T18-real-v2-ui.md; SPEC_V2 §10.5 and §12 T18. Kickoff issue #559; the five delivery units it creates.

Risks

  • A destination's action family is absent: the screen says not yet live and the live scenario is acceptance-blocked-by that slice — no local fetch is invented.
  • The five units serialise on apps/web/src/app.rs; the split is sequenced, not parallel.
  • The mock and the app can drift: the coverage instrument reads the app, so the drift is visible.
  • Bundle growth: route-level rendering and the web.yml budget are the guard.
