T19F — Decision-forum governance: project brief

Purpose

Close the two seams T19 left open: a forum policy can be created but not changed through a governed route, and the T9 board and T10 cadence rows do not name the meeting types they use. The project makes the policy a versioned release through the tenant's authoring route, and makes the board sitting and the cadence materialisation first-class consumers of T19's meeting arithmetic.

Outcome

After T19F:

  • a policy change is a reviewed authoring asset that publishes a new immutable meeting_type version; an in-flight meeting keeps the version it pinned;
  • a board subject decision can be taken in a sitting whose per-seat decisions are recorded via='meeting', with the same quorum, department, evidence and conflict rules as T19;
  • a cadence row names its meeting type and materialises exactly one meeting, idempotently;
  • the direct board path and every T19 primitive are unchanged;
  • T19F is a live programme row with its MSP/TOGAF/SAFe artefacts, evidence and a closure review that defers closure to the tranche-6 review.

Scope boundary

In: the authoring hook, the two read actions, the binding columns, the board-sitting actions, the cadence materialisation, the mock-UI and real-app panels, the programme record and the live proof.

Out: a second decision store, a chat/AV/transcript system, a rebuild of T9/T10 or the approval primitive, a change to the direct board_decide path, the T18 shell/nav, the homepage mirror, the tranche 2→4 retrospective chain (#526).

Stakeholders

Role Interest

Programme manager T19F's tranche-6 evidence and the B7 realisation increment
Programme architect / Architecture Board the sitting path, the contract, the artefact set
BCM B7's measurement across board sittings and materialised cadences
Department heads and job owners seats that are invited and counted by department
Delivery teams the unchanged direct paths and the existing meetings

Constraints

  • The authoring route is T17's; no parallel policy store.
  • meeting_type versions are immutable; a meeting pins its version.
  • The board's decision arithmetic stays T19's close_item_tx; no second calculation.
  • The cadence instance rule is a database constraint, not an action convention.
  • No local Rust compile; the compile/test verdict is preflight.yml and the PR's CI.
  • T19F stays active while its tranche is planned; closure is a programme decision.

Acceptance

The follow-on plan's P1–P5 scenarios (docs/plans/T19F-meeting-bindings.md §5) pass at the PR head or on the deployed engine; the migration ledger reaches 68; the programme record carries T19F and B7; the strict conformance publish is green with no new T19F finding.

Risks

Risk Response
A policy change is smuggled through meetings.schedule the schedule refuses a policy when the key exists; the authoring route is the only version writer
A board sitting lowers dual control close_item_tx applies the linked approval's stricter policy; the sitting adds no second path
A cadence slot gets two meetings the partial unique index on the slot; the action returns the existing instance
T17's authoring route is mid-flight (#655) the hook is additive and signature-stable; rebase or coordinate with the T17 lane
T19F closes in a planned tranche it stays active; the closure review defers closure to the tranche-6 review

Approval

The brief is reviewed with the inception, architecture, MSP and SAFe artefacts on 2026-12-01; the Approval of the plan is the programme manager's, the architecture review is the Architecture Board's.
