T19F — SAFe epic

Epic hypothesis

If a forum policy is a reviewed authoring release and the board/cadence rows name their meeting types, then every change to how the organisation decides is governed, every board decision carries its deliberation, and every cadence sitting has its meeting — measured by the B7 increment.

Leading indicator: a policy v2 published through the authoring route with v1 still pinned by an in-flight meeting; one board sitting whose decisions are via='meeting'; one cadence slot materialised once. Lagging indicator: B7's board-sitting and cadence counts at the tranche-6 review.

Non-goals

A second decision store; a chat/AV/transcript system; a rebuild of T9/T10; a change to the direct board path; a new UI destination.

Inspect and adapt

The PI review reads the live gate and the B7 increment; a policy change that cannot be published, a sitting that cannot reach quorum, or a cadence that materialises twice is a defect and a kata candidate, not a waived item.
