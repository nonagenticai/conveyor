Drivers, goals and objectives

Driver Goal Objective
A policy change today has no route a governed policy lifecycle a reviewed asset publishes a version; the schedule refuses an inline change

A cadence slot is scheduled by hand a cadence-driven forum the row materialises its meeting once

Solution concept

The authoring asset is the draft; the published asset is the release note; meeting_type is the runtime policy. The board sitting is an approval plus a meeting plus an agenda item, decided by T19's arithmetic. The cadence materialisation is a schedule with a slot and an idempotent key. Nothing else is added.

Statement of Architecture Work and compliance

The SoAW for T19F is requested, board-decided and approved in the live governance chain (scripts/governance-chain.py --project T19F), followed by a phase-F delivery contract. Two Phase-G compliance reviews record the level and the follow-ups. The review checks: every policy version is published through the authoring gate; every sitting decision is a via='meeting' decision row; every materialised cadence has one non-cancelled meeting; the direct board path is unchanged.
