T19F — MSP profile

Business case

T19 delivered the meeting as the decision forum; its policy could not be changed through a governance route and its two consumers — the T9 board and the T10 cadence — did not name the type they use. T19F closes both seams by reusing the built authoring route and T19's arithmetic. The cost is one migration, five actions, two test files and two UI panels; the alternative is a policy that can only be changed by a redeploy and a board whose decisions are not seated.

Benefit B7 — Deliberated cross-department decisions

B7's existing baseline (seed/programme-definition.json) is a measurement record: zero deliberated decision rows before T19. T19F does not overwrite it. It adds the realisation increment:

Measure Before T19F After T19F
Board subject decisions with per-seat positions 0 (direct approval rows only) a query over approval_decision.via for surface='meeting'
Cadence slots with a materialised meeting 0 a query over meeting.cadence_slot
Policy versions with an authoring provenance 0 authoring_asset.published_meeting_type_id

The measurement method is the existing read-time aggregate over meeting, meeting_agenda_item, meeting_attendance, meeting_position, approval_decision and project_dependency; T19F adds the two joins (the authored policy and the cadence slot) and writes nothing at read time.
