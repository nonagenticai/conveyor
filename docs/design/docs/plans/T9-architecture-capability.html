T9 — TOGAF ADM capability

Programme project: T9 · issue #81

Gate (SPEC_V2 §12 T9): an architecture project passes Preliminary and A–F with each phase's mandatory artefacts approved and its contract live; a phase missing a mandatory artefact is refused; a delivery project without a live contract is refused at design; a compliance review records one level and a waiver names its owner and review date; an H review produces exactly one change or kata.

1. Objective

The conveyor runs an architecture capability, not a document library. The Preliminary phase establishes the capability — principles, the tailored ADM profile, the artefact standard and repository, the Architecture Board, governance and contracts, maturity and skills. An architecture project then walks phases A–H as WBS nodes, each phase carrying the mandatory artefacts its profile names and closing on an approved architecture_contract. Every artefact is a typed row — catalog, matrix or diagram — with the standard's common metadata, queryable entries and trace links, so the repository answers what it derives from, realises and reuses. The conveyor buildout itself runs under this capability: P0 is the Preliminary-phase and programme-definition project; T1–T17 are the delivery projects the phase-G reviews govern.

4. The model

4.1 The artefact standard

architecture_artifact is the parent. Its type is catalog, matrix or diagram; all three inherit the common metadata: title, status, version, author and date, reviewer and review date, the related change initiative or project, purpose/stakeholders/scope, and the published document or contract. Type-specific metadata: a catalog names its unique identifier, item name and description of use or structure; a matrix names its axes (usually two); a diagram names its building block/metamodel and viewpoint.

Entries are rows (architecture_artifact_entry: axis_a, axis_b, value). A catalog uses one axis; a matrix uses two. A diagram's content is a document; the row carries the metadata and the viewpoint, not the pixels.

Each artefact names its phase_code (Preliminary, A–H) and its plateau — psa (Project Start Architecture), hld (High-Level Design) or sd (Solution Design). The profile decides which plateau a phase requires. architecture_artifact_link carries derivation, realization, reuse and dependency edges between artefacts; architecture.repository.search answers reuse across projects, phases and plateaus.

4.2 The capability (Preliminary phase)

adm_profile holds the tenant's tailoring: the phase set, the mandatory artefact types per phase and plateau, and the contract kind that closes each phase. artifact_type is the seeded standard (§4.1's catalogue with phase, plateau, type and mandatory flag); the profile filters it, so a young capability carries fewer artefacts and the set grows with the assessed maturity.

governance_body(kind='architecture_board') holds the board's membership, charter, commissioning authority and decision limits. It is a row with a lifecycle: architecture.board_open opens it (idempotent on its stable key — a second open returns the commissioned body, never overwrites its charter), architecture.board_get reads it by id or key, and architecture.board_decide records a board decision with its evidence. The principles catalog is an ordinary artefact (type='principles_catalog') whose entries carry statement, rationale and implications. architecture_maturity scores nine dimensions on 0–5 per stage with a baseline and milestones; architecture_skill_assessment scores the seven skills categories per role.

4.3 The phases and the contracts

An architecture project materialises Preliminary and A–H as wbs_node(kind='phase') rows. architecture.phases.advance refuses unless every artefact the profile marks mandatory for that phase and plateau is approved, every artefact link the standard requires resolves, and the phase's contract is live; the refusal names the missing artefact or the contract's state, and writes no contract row.

architecture_contract carries four kinds across the ADM: statement_of_architecture_work (Phase A — assignment, scope, responsibilities, acceptance criteria; distinct from the designs produced under it), delivery_contract (after F — delivery commitments and the method for assessing compliance), statement_of_intent (at completion — intended use, ownership, measures of effectiveness) and request_for_architecture (Phase H — further architecture work). A contract change is a new version and re-opens its gate; the approved body is immutable.

4.4 Delivery compliance and change management

architecture_compliance_review records exactly one level per delivery project — irrelevant, consistent, compliant, conformant, fully_conformant or non_conformant — with findings and evidence refs. A deviation is rejected, mitigated, or accepted through architecture_waiver for a defined period; the waiver names the accepting asset owner, the reason, the additional cost and risk, and the reassessment date, and is refused without an owner and a date.

A Phase-H review records exactly one typed outcome: a project_register_item(kind='change') with its impact block and the change gate, or a kata candidate routed to the kata lane. A review with no recorded verdict cannot close the phase. A request_for_architecture is the third artifact of a change review — it opens further architecture work when the current architecture no longer meets its purpose.

The delivery design gate resolves only against a live approved contract trace-linked to the delivery project; absent, draft or superseded all refuse, and no approval row is written.

6. Data model

The eleven tables are in SPEC_V2 §10.3. New columns only where unavoidable: project.project_type_code already exists; governed_asset.kind is extended where a definition artefact is generic (charter, closure, mandate, brief, blueprint, business case, refined vision, benefits strategy, realization plan, projects dossier), never for an architecture artefact or contract, which have their own tables. All tables are tenant-scoped and ENABLE+FORCE RLS.

7. Actions and events

The architecture.* family is declared in SPEC_V2 §10.4. Every action is an ActionSpec with one declaration → HTTP + MCP + OpenAPI + generated client. The events are one subject and one verb (architecture_artifact.changed, architecture_phase.closed, compliance_review.recorded, architecture_waiver.granted). A phase advance and its contract approval commit in one transaction with their event.

8. The programme

seed/programme.json T9 carries the §12 gate text; its expected outputs come from the output standard. P0's class carries the definition categories; T9's class carries the architecture categories. The seed is generated (scripts/build-programme-seed.py), never hand-edited, and --check is the gate.
