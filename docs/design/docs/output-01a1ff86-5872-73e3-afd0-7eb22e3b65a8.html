Project brief — S02 Skeleton (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Outcome

One skeleton that every later slice inherits: a ten-member Rust workspace, eleven platform tables with forced row-level security, OIDC and dev principals, a fail-closed licence gate, a fenced job lease that survives a worker death, a replayable SSE stream and an append-only audit. The outcome is proven by the plan's §7 gate, not asserted: the tenant-isolation, kill, two-worker, replay, audit and licence proofs each have a command.

Boundary

In: the workspace and its two apps, the eleven platform tables, the auth and licence boundary, the job runner and the SSE stream, the audit, health and the served contract. Out: domain aggregates (S03), the action registry (S04), board and approval screens (S03–S06), the Keycloak realm and deploy (S14), pgvector (S07), the UI (S12) and the git repository itself.

Reuse sources

The plan names three local sources: template-rust-service at 0c7396a, agent at 83b8de7 and strata_rag at b9426c3. The skeleton reuses their workspace, pool and migrator patterns and their test-harness shape; it does not vendor them. SQLx, axum and the problem+json error shape are the shared stack.

Inception units

S00's signed parity inventory is the surface list; S01's UI ADR reserves apps/web without building it. The eleven tables and five crates are the inception units S03, S04 and S06 consume unchanged.

Dependencies

S00 (needs) fixes the surfaces; Postgres 18 is the record; Redis Streams is the nudge; Keycloak is the OIDC issuer S02 consumes; the licences service supplies the entitlement row. None of these is configured here.

Acceptance

acceptance/programme/S02.toml is the executable acceptance: project closed, approved baseline, charter asset and approved charter gate, declared closure outputs, board-approved delivery contract, compliant Phase-G review distinct from the requester, no expired waiver, PI cadence/plan/objectives, WSJF on the WBS deliverable, a demo credit, a lesson, no open P0 package, the artefact standard and the rework package. The receipt is docs/evidence/rework/S02.json.

Capabilities

Tenancy isolation, identity and access, job execution, realtime events, audit and evidence, entitlement enforcement and operability. Each is carried by named components and named tables, and each has a gate.

Plan

docs/plans/slices/S02-skeleton.md: steps 0–14 from the workspace through the tables, auth, jobs, SSE, audit and the served contract. The retrospective re-reads them as delivered; the two-worker proof is the one step the earlier issue left open and this rework closes.
