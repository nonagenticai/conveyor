PI plan — tranche 2 (retrospective, 2026-11-21)

Tranche 2 covers S03 (Domain and schema) and T2 (Decision ladder). Both are already delivered as code; this increment records their programme evidence and burns down what the audit found. It is planned now and executed now, so the plan is retrospective in the same sense as the tranche-0 and tranche-1 sets.

Objectives

  • O1 — S03 is re-recorded and closed on its own manifest. Charter, Statement of Architecture Work, the seven Phase A–D artefacts, the delivery contract, the Phase-G review, the two expected outputs and the closure review all exist and are published; acceptance/programme/S03.toml is green at the closing snapshot. Its two audit findings are recorded and owned; a P1 does not block closure under this programme's P0-only product gate.
  • O2 — T2's programme record exists and names its blocking gate. The same record is produced for T2, and its Phase-G review states that v1-t2-1 (a generated draft attached by documents.compose with no draft.apply decision) blocks closure. T2 stays open; its status is a recorded consequence of that gate, not an omission.
  • O3 — the tranche is reviewed on evidence. The review reads each project's manifest result at the closing snapshot and records a typed outcome: continue if both objectives are met, pause where one is blocked.

The work-in-progress limit is three packages: two code packages and one lifecycle record. At the time of planning, S03's lifecycle and T2's record are the two in-flight records, and v1-t2-1 is the only code package in flight. The remaining four packages are queued behind it and are not started until it lands, because each of them touches the same ladder surface.
