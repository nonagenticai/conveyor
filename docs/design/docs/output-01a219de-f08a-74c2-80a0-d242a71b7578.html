Entries

The catalog states two drivers, two goals and five objectives. D1 and D2 explain why a decision needs its deliberation: the trade-offs between departments, the conditions attached, the dissent and the commitments given. G1 and G2 fix the shape of the answer — a meeting is a card, agenda items resolve approvals, and the outcome is arithmetic over typed positions under the stricter policy. O1 to O5 are the observable objectives: per-seat approval_decision rows with via='meeting', inquorate deferral, immutable meeting_type versions, the owner deciding live and asynchronously, and a forum with no decision being flagged.

The catalog is deliberately short: it fixes the chain from driver to observable objective and refuses every adjacent goal the slice does not take. There is no chat objective, no transcript store, no calendar dependency and no LLM that selects, decides or infers. Each objective names the row a reviewer can query, so a future review can test the goal without re-reading the plan.
