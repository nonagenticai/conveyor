Entry and exit criteria

  • Entry. Tranche 0 is entered on the programme mandate and P0's existence. Every later tranche is entered only when the previous tranche's review records continue; a pause or stop holds the chain at that tranche (P0 plan §7).
  • Exit. A tranche closes when every project in it meets its acceptance with published evidence and the review records one of continue, pause or stop. A review with no evidence is refused; a re-baseline goes through change control, never through the review itself.
  • The first tranche is authorised only after the definition set is accepted, and no delivery project starts before that authorisation (P0 plan §7).

Review dates

Not set. The programme_tranche.review_at field is null for every row: the sponsor dates the reviews at P0's acceptance. The plan names the sequence and the criteria; it does not invent dates. The end-of-tranche review's evidence is the baseline, the benefit status against the profiles and the risk and issue register.

Sequencing

The order is the dependency levels of docs/SPEC_V2.md §12, measured by the coverage: tranche 0 is the programme definition (P0) and the platform/design foundations; tranche 1 the plan spine and the skeleton; each later tranche the projects whose prerequisites the earlier ones delivered. The programme does not need all 48 projects before it produces value; the definition gate prevents building the wrong 47.

Gap. The tranche rows and this plan are in place; the review that authorises tranche 0 is the sponsor's decision and is not yet taken. tranche-review is a visible gap in P0's checklist until the owner records it; the session does not fabricate a review.
