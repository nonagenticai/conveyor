Project brief — T11 metrics, metering and maturity (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Outcome

Every flow, quality, cost and governance figure the conveyor reports is a read-time computation over its own rows; spend rolls up at tenant, department, programme, project, job, run, step and call; C0–C5 maturity is decided by versioned threshold configuration; and the derived cost reconciles with billing_service exactly at six decimals or opens an incident. The console Metering screen reads the published snapshot's measure.inference aggregates and shows no fixture number when the section is absent.

Boundary

Out of scope: charging, which remains with billing_service; the eval- and benchmark-backed quality metrics — first-pass yield, rework rate, defect escapes, eval score and benchmark delta — whose ledgers S17–S20 own; knowledge metrics whose ledgers do not exist (T8); and per-tool health and retirement. T11 must not fork a stored metric or a stored cost: a metrics read writes no metric row, the migration adds only metric_threshold and metric_reconciliation, and the console never recomputes cost from a stale cache.

Reuse sources

The measurement reads the conveyor's existing ledgers rather than a new store: inference_call and tool_call for tokens and spend; run and run_step for attribution and drill-down; approval for governance; card, list and event for flow; job_binding, job_department and department for organisation attribution; benefit_profile, benefit_transition, benefit_link and project_dependency for MSP metrics; and commercial_price for the derived cost. It adds only the versioned threshold configuration, the reconciliation verdict, the eight actions and the console unit.

Inception units

T11 is the measurement slice and has no separate inception analysis or architecture spike: its inputs are the metric definitions in SPEC_V2 §7.5 and the ledgers named above. The retrospective reconstitutes the governance record over the completed work — charter, Statement of Architecture Work, the seven A–D artefacts approved by the board, the delivery contract, the WBS deliverable, the Phase-G review, this brief, the evidence page and the closure review — against the 2026-11-21 rework and the 2026-11-22 artefact publications.

Dependencies

The project row records depends_on as needs P0, the programme definition. It is related to S02 and T1, the other tranche-1 projects, and it blocks T12, which follows all V2 slices. Operationally every figure depends on its ledger being written: a missing ledger is reported as unavailable with a reason, never as zero. The reconciliation depends on billing.summary's attested total, and the console depends on the published snapshot carrying measure.inference. In governance the pack rests on the standing delegation 01a1ff1f-e4ba-72dc-9c1a-13049933cf14, the approved scope baseline 01a1ff8f-47cb-78cf-9db8-d98d863e7dd0 and the delivery contract 01a1ff8f-08ee-7bc5-bb39-81304d03c4ef.

Acceptance

The SPEC_V2 §12 gate is the contract: every metric and every metering level recomputes from rows; the reconciliation with billing_service is exact or opens an incident; a C0–C5 level changes only when the measured evidence crosses its recorded threshold; a gate that never refused is flagged. The executable form is acceptance/programme/T11.toml run by scripts/verify-project.py against the published snapshot, backed by the eight metrics integration tests, metric_thresholds_are_tenant_isolated and the console checks 9b–9d. The Phase-G review 01a1ff90-4e19-7bcf-acbf-3f29d980572e records the level compliant.

Capabilities

The eight metrics actions — metrics.flow, metrics.job, metrics.usage, metrics.programme, metrics.maturity, metrics.reconcile, metrics.threshold_get and metrics.threshold_update — deliver the five metric families, the eight metering levels and the run, step and call drill-down, the contiguous C0–C5 ladder over versioned thresholds, the six-decimal billing reconciliation with an incident on divergence, and the console Metering screen over the snapshot's live inference aggregates. The two T11 tables are tenant-isolated, and the nine-name evidence vocabulary is closed.

Plan

The plan is docs/SPEC_V2.md §12 (T11) against §7.5 and §10.4. The metrics capability was delivered by issue #84, merged 2026-11-19, and the console unit by issue #458, merged 2026-11-20. The governance was reconstituted and the project re-closed on 2026-11-21; the seven artefact documents were published on 2026-11-22.
