Project brief — T11 metrics, metering and maturity

Outcome

Every flow, quality, cost and governance figure the conveyor reports is a read-time computation over its own rows; spend rolls up at eight organisational levels; C0–C5 maturity is decided by versioned threshold configuration; and billing reconciles exactly or opens an incident.

Boundary

Out of scope: charging — billing_service remains the charging system; eval- and benchmark-backed quality metrics (first-pass yield, rework rate, defect escapes, eval score and benchmark delta), whose ledgers belong to S17–S20; knowledge metrics whose ledgers do not exist (T8); and per-tool health and retirement (SPEC_V2 §11.9, the tool platform). The console reads the published snapshot; it never recomputes cost from a stale cache.

Reuse sources

  • docs/SPEC_V2.md §7.5 (metrics, metering and the ledger), §10.4 (the metrics.* family) and §12 (T11).
  • crates/domain/src/metrics.rs, crates/store/src/metrics.rs and crates/actions/src/metrics/mod.rs — the pure rules, the read models and the eight actions.
  • migrations/0059_metrics.sql — the threshold configuration and the reconciliation record.
  • seed/catalogue.json — the incident card type; served-spec.lock.json — the served contracts.
  • docs/design/v2.js meteringLive() and the snapshot's measure.inference (#458).

Inception units

None. T11 is the measurement slice; its inputs are the ledgers and the metric definitions in SPEC_V2 §7.5, not an inception analysis or architecture spike.

Dependencies

blocked-by: P0 (the programme definition; meta.depends_on). related: S02 and T1, the other tranche-1 projects. blocks: T12 (one organisation loop, which follows all V2 slices per SPEC_V2 §12).

Acceptance

The SPEC_V2 §12 gate, verbatim: "every metric and every metering level recomputes from rows; the reconciliation with billing_service is exact or opens an incident; a C0–C5 level changes only when the measured evidence crosses its recorded threshold; a gate that never refused is flagged". It is executed by acceptance/programme/T11.toml through scripts/verify-project.py against the published snapshot, together with the governance checks listed in the delivery contract.

Capabilities

The eight metrics.* actions, the eight metering levels and the run drill-down, the C0–C5 threshold configuration, the billing reconciliation with an incident on divergence, and the console Metering screen over the snapshot's live inference aggregates.
