T19F — Project brief

One page: what this project changes, what it reuses, and how it is accepted.

Outcome

After T19F, a forum policy changes only through the reviewed authoring route (publishing a new immutable meeting_type version while an in-flight meeting keeps its pinned version), a board subject decision is taken in a sitting whose per-seat decisions are recorded via='meeting', and a cadence row materialises exactly one meeting — with T19's primitives and the direct board path unchanged.

Boundary

In: the authoring publish hook and the two policy reads, the governance_body and programme_cadence bindings, the board-sitting actions, the cadence materialisation, the mock-UI and real-app panels, the programme record and the live proof.

Out: a second decision store, a chat/AV/transcript system, a rebuild of T9/T10 or the approval primitive, a change to the direct board_decide path, the T18 shell/nav, the homepage mirror, the tranche 2→4 retrospective chain (#526), and programme-wide conformance debt.

Reuse sources

  • T17's authoring route (authoring.intake/draft/review/publish) — the only policy writer.
  • T19's meeting aggregate and close_item_tx — the only decision arithmetic.
  • T9's governance_body — the sitting's convener and body.
  • T10's programme_cadence — the materialised forum.
  • docs/SPEC_V2.md §6.8 and §12 T19F — the gate.

Inception units

  • docs/plans/T19/closure-review.md — the two open items this project closes.
  • docs/plans/T19F/architecture-design.md — PSA + HLD.

Acceptance

  • Given a live meeting pinned to v1, when a reviewed policy asset publishes v2, then the meeting still resolves v1 and the type list carries both versions.
  • Given a board subject and a bound forum type, when the sitting's chair records a position and the item is decided, then the approval carries one via='meeting' decision row per seat.
  • Given a cadence row with a type, when materialise runs twice, then exactly one non-cancelled meeting exists with the bound type and the slot.
  • Given T19F's record, when the governance chain checks, then seven artefacts, both contracts, both Phase-G reviews, B7 read-not-overwritten and the published architecture-artifact exist.

Capabilities

  • B1 Governed tenant assets — T17's route extended with the policy payload.
  • B2 Deliberated decisions — T19's meeting reused by the board sitting.
  • B3 Cadence-driven forums — T10's cadence row bound to a meeting type.
