Project Brief — SPEC-V2 (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Outcome

SPEC-V2 delivers docs/SPEC_V2.md, the conveyor's organisational operating model, and publishes it as the spec output: an organisation spine of industry → department(s) → job with an immutable release and a tenant-scoped binding, a work hierarchy from programme to run, a four-rung decision ladder, and TOGAF, SAFe, PMP and MSP implemented as rows and gates rather than documents. The outcome the plan states in §13 is that the organisation's work becomes auditable at the level of a single decision — which option, which confidence, which policy, which person, which evidence.

Boundary

Out of scope are V1's S-slices, which are not re-opened; the V2 delivery slices T0–T19 and T19F, which are their own projects; and docs/SPEC_LEGACY_KAG.md, which owns the legacy-kag and w2v rewrites (D54). The model explicitly does not do customer care (D48), does not import a manifest from another system (D33), and does not claim TOGAF certification, SAFe compliance, PMP certification, MSP conformance or full ADM conformance (§8.6).

Reuse sources

The plan reuses the ideas of the agent platform's KH model (D106–D115) and reimplements them, so there is no runtime import (D33). It reuses V1's Rust substrate, its capability → procedure → step → action or gate knowledge, its run, approval, action_invocation and event memory, its capability_agent executor configuration, its inference ledger and its eval and benchmark instruments. The retrospective record reuses the snapshot's approved A–D rows and the rework note's residuals.

Inception units

The delivery units are the V2 slices: T0 to T3 and T13 in the first three months, T4 to T12 and T14 to T19 as the following work, and T19F as the measured follow-on (§12). The retrospective reconstitution of this project's own lifecycle is the #526 unit, whose 38 acts are recorded in transcript.jsonl and read back one write at a time by programme-record.py. Units T9 and T10 are measured built on 2026-11-23, and T19F on 2026-11-24.
