Decision

Pause. The tranche's exit criterion — every project meets its acceptance with published evidence — is not met: both manifests are red, and the two closures are refused by the product's own gate. Continuing to tranche 3 while tranche 2's projects sit open would be the pacing violation the programme exists to prevent.

Conditions to resume: (1) the four P0 manifest failures per project are fixed and the manifests re-run green; (2) the two projects close, which requires either a Phase-G review at a conformant level or a waiver signed by a real accepting owner; (3) tranche 2's retro records one lesson per finding.
