Concerns and sensitivities

  • The sponsor carries the benefits and the acceptance; a number that was not measured is the sensitivity, not a missed target.
  • The maintainer sessions build and evidence; they do not approve their own work, and the publish decision remains the owner's.
  • Outside contributors need a design they can review and a path they can follow; the sensitivity is opacity, not disagreement.
  • The organisation's jobs need releases and bindings; a stale binding is refused by the system.
  • Reviewers need evidence; an overstated completion is the failure they exist to catch.
  • The public reader needs the programme to be readable and stable; a 404 on a published output is the defect that breaks the contract.

Influence and interest

The sponsor and the maintainer sessions have high influence and high interest; the reviewers have high importance with medium influence; the outside contributors and the organisation's jobs are the medium band; the public reader is low influence but a real interest. The exposure column in the row records how much each group is exposed to a delivery failure.

Support and engagement

Support is champion for the owner, the sessions and the reviewers; positive for contributors and readers; neutral for the organisation's jobs (the positions are seeded, not yet operating). The engagement target per group is the row's engagement_target; the trend is unknown where no cycle has run; where a group has been consistently engaged (the sponsor, the sessions, the reviewers) the row records steady as a dated judgement, not a measured trend.

Benefits and disbenefits

The benefits each group receives are the vision's outcomes (O1–O6) through the benefits map; the disbenefits are real costs (the gates' discipline, the review cycle, the transition from code paths to positions) and are named rather than denied.

Gap. The BCM position is unstaffed; the programme manager is the interim owner of these profiles and must hand them to the BCM when the position is staffed (skills assessment SA-1's S1 milestone).
