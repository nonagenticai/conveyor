Stakeholder profiles — P0 (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Stakeholders

The profiles published on 2026-11-18 at 08:17:48 (01a1e9e1-d583-74cc-bc8c-097fbe79c1ed) cover the six stakeholder groups of the programme and their engagement. The set is the programme-level view the stakeholder map and the governance roles rest on, and it is adopted with the definition set rather than maintained as a separate register.

Concerns and sensitivities

For each of the six groups the profiles record concerns and sensitivities, together with importance, influence, interest and exposure as plan §3 item 9 requires. The P0 brief does not enumerate the individual concerns, so this retrospective does not restate them as if it did. What is measurable is that the fields are populated per group in the published profiles.

Influence and interest

The profiles record each group's importance, influence, interest and exposure, which is what lets the programme decide who is consulted, who is informed and who decides. Influence and interest are recorded per group rather than averaged into a single stakeholder score. The P0 record defines those fields; no engagement outcome is measured on 2026-11-26.

Support and engagement

Each profile carries the group's support level and its trend, plus the engagement target the programme intends to reach. That makes support a managed variable rather than an assumption in the business case. What is measurable now is the recorded target per group; actual movement in support is not measured by P0.

Benefits and disbenefits

The profiles distribute benefits and disbenefits to each group, so the programme can see who gains and who carries the cost of changed practice. This is the stakeholder side of the benefits map, and it is why the map records disbenefits as well as benefits. The distribution is defined in the published profiles; no group's realised benefit is claimed.
