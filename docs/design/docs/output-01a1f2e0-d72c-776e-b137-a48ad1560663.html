Architecture waiver — T9 TOGAF ADM capability (retrospective, 2026-11-22)

Deviation

No deviation is waived in this rework. The waiver register for T9 is empty: the Phase-G review records compliant with named deviations, and the six open findings (v1-p81-4, v1-p81-5, v1-p81-6, v1-w2a-3, v1-w2a-4 and v1-w2a-6) are carried as work packages with acceptance clauses rather than accepted as deviations from the T9 delivery contract. The one clause that is not proved, the Phase-H exactly-one rule, is a finding to fix, not a capability the programme chooses to live without.

Accepting owner

If a waiver were granted, it would have to name the accepting asset owner and the scope of that owner's authority. None is named here, because no waiver row is created. The Architecture Board (conveyor-architecture-board) is the approving body for the capability, but it is not the accepting owner of a deviation: the waiver rule of SPEC_V2 §8.1 places the acceptance with the asset owner, and the action refuses a waiver without one.

Reason and impact

No reason or impact is recorded because no deviation is accepted. The impact of not waiving is explicit: the open findings stay visible in the ledger and in the Phase-G findings, and the project closes on the P0-only product gate with the P1/P2 packages still owned. The alternative, a dated waiver for the Phase-H clause, would hide the missing proof behind an acceptance the review cannot honestly support.

Reassessment

No reassessment date is set, for the same reason. The rule the empty register enforces is proved by crates/api/tests/architecture_compliance.rs::one_level_and_a_dated_waiver: a waiver without an owner or a review date is refused. When a future review does accept a deviation, it must name the owner, the reason, the additional cost and risk, and the reassessment date; until then this document records the absence.
