Architecture design — T9 TOGAF ADM capability (retrospective, 2026-11-22)

Context and boundary

This design adds the architecture capability of SPEC_V2 §8.1 to the blueprint: a typed repository, a tailored ADM profile, the Architecture Board, four contract kinds, the compliance review and waiver register, and the two capability assessments. It owns the eleven tables of §10.3 and the 26 architecture.* actions of §10.4. It must not duplicate governed_asset (requirements, principles, ADRs), trace_link, the approval primitive, document, wbs_node or the PM job; plan §2 names each as reused, not rebuilt.

Aggregates, tables and actions

The tables are artifact_type, architecture_artifact, architecture_artifact_entry, architecture_artifact_link, adm_profile, architecture_contract, architecture_compliance_review, architecture_waiver, governance_body, architecture_maturity and architecture_skill_assessment. The actions are the 26 architecture.* declarations of §10.4, from artifacts_list to profile_tailor. The events are architecture_artifact.changed, architecture_phase.closed, compliance_review.recorded and architecture_waiver.granted. A phase advance and its contract approval commit in one transaction with their event (plan §7).

Decisions

An artefact is a typed row, not a file; a document is its content, not its identity. The profile filters the seeded standard, so a young capability carries fewer mandatory artefacts and the set grows with assessed maturity. A contract is a gate object with a version; the approved body is immutable and a change re-opens the gate. A review records exactly one compliance level, and a waiver must name its owner and reassessment date. Phase H produces exactly one change or kata. The open findings are the places these decisions are not fully enforced.

Acceptance

The D1–D4 scenarios are proved by the named tests in architecture_artifacts.rs, architecture_capability.rs, togaf_architecture_phases.rs, togaf_delivery_design_gate.rs and architecture_compliance.rs. The D5 clause is partial: v1-p81-6 and the absent a_systemic_finding_produces_a_kata_candidate are recorded in the review.

Architecture artefacts

The seven Phase A–D artefacts this rework publishes are the stakeholder map, the driver/goal/objective catalog, the requirements catalog and the solution concept (A), the business capability map (B), the data entity catalog (C) and the technology standards catalog (D), owned by the programme architect and reviewed by the Architecture Board on 2026-11-22.

Migration and rollout

The slice ships one forward-only migration adding the eleven tables; the plan names the tables, not the file, and this rework does not invent a filename. Rollout is the existing binary and schema: no new port (§10.6). The console destination is declared in §10.5.
