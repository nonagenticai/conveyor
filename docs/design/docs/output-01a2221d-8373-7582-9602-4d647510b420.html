Architecture design — T3 PM job (retrospective, 2026-11-28)

Context and boundary

This design owns the PM job: the project's lifecycle actions and the gates between them. It must not duplicate the approval engine, the store, the cadence surfaces or the registers; it sequences them and refuses a step whose precondition is not met. The blueprint it fits is the one the requirements catalogue and the solution concept name: one project run end to end, with the approver kept distinct from the requester and a baseline change forced through a change request.

Aggregates, tables and actions

  • Aggregates: the project, the WBS node, the scope baseline, the approval and its decision, the register item and the cadence row.
  • Tables: the project and WBS rows, the approval and decision rows, the register rows and the baseline rows — the same rows the close gate reads.
  • Actions: projects.charter, projects.baseline, projects.close, approvals.request, approvals.decide, the cadence actions and the retrospective actions; a baseline change is changes.request and a register row, never an edit.
  • Events: each gate decision, the demo credit per accepted deliverable, the PI plan approval and the tranche review.

Decisions

  • The approver must differ from the requester; a rule decision is accepted only under a published delegation naming the scope, and the decision records decided_by_kind.
  • A baseline change requires a change request naming the baseline; the frozen row is superseded, never edited.
  • Closure reads the declared outputs, the closure review, the live delivery contract and its Phase-G review, the approved scope baseline, the seven A–D artefacts and their registers, the WBS WSJF inputs and a demo credit per accepted deliverable.
  • The charter gate and the closure gate are separate decisions; a single approval cannot stand for both.

Acceptance

acceptance/programme/T3.toml carries the gate, and SPEC_V2 §12 states it in prose. The proof is the cadence and gate suites — crates/store/tests/programme_cadence.rs, refinement.rs, demo_gate.rs, retro.rs, tranche_review.rs and benefit_transition.rs, with crates/actions/tests/pi_plan_approval.rs, demo_credit.rs and apps/server/tests/cadence_jobs.rs — and the record's own closure: the project's closure review is published and its closure gate was decided by an approver distinct from its requester.

Architecture artefacts

The design's record is the seven Phase A–D artefacts — stakeholder map, driver/goal/objective catalogue, requirements catalog, solution concept, business capability map, data entity catalogue and technology standards — together with the five register kinds and the WBS deliverable. Each artefact is a typed row with its entries and links and is published as its own page.
