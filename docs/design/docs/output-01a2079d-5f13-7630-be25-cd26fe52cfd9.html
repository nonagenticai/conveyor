Inspect and Adapt — conveyor buildout, tranche 3

The tranche-3 retro: what went well, what did not, the lessons recorded on the retro cadence, the actions they produce and the kata candidates they raise.

What went well

  • The three registers are operated, not merely modelled: fourteen risks with owners and review dates, fifty-seven ADR rows seeded through the decisions actions, and B1–B7 baselined with B1/B2/B4/B5/B6 active under approved changes.
  • The clean-build discipline produced a trustworthy binary and lock at 9ac63b41 (548 actions, 579 operations, --check-lock OK) after the shared target had polluted an earlier build.
  • The delegated rule path worked on every programme gate it was used on: PI plan, tranche review, change approvals, all recorded rule with a published via.delegation_ref.

What did not

  • The promotion journal lost its last row when a trim by line count ignored a header object; the --check grammar caught it, but only after the fact.
  • The first lock was generated from a binary built against a stale shared target and had to be regenerated from a clean build.
  • A benefit baseline edit resets the profile to draft; activation is a second recorded write, which cost an extra version on every profile.
  • The schema-drift kata cannot open: a destination 422 has no eval/benchmark instrument, and T5 refuses an unmeasurable kata.

Lessons

  • k1. A clean build before a lock regeneration is the only trustworthy verdict.
  • k2. The promotion journal must be validated after every append.
  • k3. A delegation document's owner project is part of its contract; the guard must accept every owner the delegation actually uses.
  • k4. A measurement change resets a benefit profile to draft; activation is its own write.
  • k5. A defect with no instrument cannot open a kata; re-anchor rather than fabricate.

Actions

  • v1-w4a-5 proceeds as a deliberate slice (timeout from timeout_secs, pending + resume_token, a status tool).
  • promote-record.py gains decisions.create/decisions.transition replay before the deploy-bound promotion.
  • The three programme screens (Risk, Decisions, Benefits evaluation) and the per-project factory cards follow in tranche 4.

Kata candidates

The schema-drift kata is a candidate under T5, contingent on the contract-test instrument class. No kata row is opened until that instrument exists; the finding is re-anchored to T5 and stays open.
