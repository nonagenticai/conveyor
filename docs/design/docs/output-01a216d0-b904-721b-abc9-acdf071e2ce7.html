Benefits Diagram — T9 TOGAF ADM capability

Goals and outcomes

  • Phases pass in order. An architecture project passes Preliminary and A–F only when each phase's mandatory artefacts are approved and its contract is live (togaf_architecture_phases.rs::phases_a_to_f_pass_in_order).
  • A missing artefact blocks its phase. An unapproved artefact refuses the phase.
  • No design without a live contract. A delivery project without one is refused at design (togaf_delivery_design_gate.rs).
  • One level, dated waivers. A compliance review records exactly one level, and a waiver names its owner and review date (architecture_compliance.rs).
  • H produces one governed outcome. A scope change produces a change register row; a systemic finding produces a kata with no change row; an unrecorded review refuses the gate.

Benefits and sub-benefits

The capability is the programme's design-control surface: contracts bind before design, gates refuse unapproved artefacts, compliance levels are single-valued and waivers are owned and dated. Its sub-benefits: no design proceeds on a draft contract, no phase passes on a missing artefact, and every deviation has an owner and a reassessment date.
