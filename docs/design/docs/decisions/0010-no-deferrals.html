0010 — No deferrals; every finding executes

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-11-22 · Slice: #526 (programme)

Context

The programme was disposing of open work with dated instruments rather than executing it: v1-s14-1 carried a waiver accepted 2026-11-22 and expiring 2027-02-18; the acceptance manifest acceptance/programme/P0.toml carried an [[allow]] for benefit_baseline expiring 2027-02-18; verify-viability.py refused the programme unless every open finding carried a valid deferral; and verify-project.py and audit-programme-conformance.py suppressed non-conformance through active waivers. The measured board at 58645942 shows what that bought: 12 green / 35 red manifests, 14 closed-on-red projects, 965 artefacts of which 418 are absent and 311 partial, 483 standards findings, 102 audit packages, 62 contract gaps, and 6 of 6 viability checks missing.

On 2026-11-22 the owner directed that all dated deferrals are no longer true: the programme defers nothing; it executes the work, and that decision and approval authority sits with the Claude sessions, exercised with rigour, grounding, and captured and published evidence.

Decision

  1. No deferral, waiver, [[allow]] or dated residual is a valid disposition for any #526 finding or artefact. Every finding is executed.
  2. Where a proof cannot exist because the system it proved has been decommissioned, the finding's claim is corrected to what the surviving system can prove, with the decommissioning as its evidence. A correction is not a waiver and has no expiry.
  3. The instruments refuse suppression: verify-viability.py, verify-project.py, audit-programme-conformance.py and governance/conformance.json treat a waiver or deferral on programme work as a finding. The TOGAF architecture_waiver remains legal for a deviation from an architecture specification (SPEC_V2 §8.1), requires an accepting owner and a reassessment date, and the programme carries zero active ones at green.
  4. Decisions are rule decisions with a resolving via.delegation_ref, published with their rationale and evidence. No gate is claimed as human.
  5. The superseded decision and its register row are kept; history is forward-only.

Consequences

  • The findings ledger re-anchors v1-s14-1 to decision document 01a2029c-9254-7605-8a7c-3013b28ee794; its waiver is removed and the finding stays open until it is executed or its claim corrected.
  • Removing suppression turns the gates red-first (98 open findings, 539 packages). The gate stays red until the work clears it; no green is claimed in the meantime.
  • Every tranche review from tranche 3 cites this ADR for its no-deferral verdict.
  • The acceptance standard (v5 §5, clauses (a)–(j)) is the close gate, and its own change row U5-ACCEPTANCE-STANDARD (01a2029f-8e2e-78de-bc99-119da42fdfbf) stays open until the change gate can be approved (blocked by v1-change-1).

Not claimed
