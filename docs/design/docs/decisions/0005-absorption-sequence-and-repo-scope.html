0005 — Absorption sequence, strata stays external, and conveyor-executed rewrites

Status: Accepted · Date: 2026-11-13 · Issue: #66

Decision

  1. The absorption sequence is fixed: inference-router (#44) → licenses (#45) → billing-service (#46). The V2 product spine (T1–T3, T13) is queued behind them; it is not the current thrust.
  2. strata_ingest and strata_rag stay separate repositories. They are already Rust, they are shared with the agent platform, and their release cadence and open-sourcing are decided separately. The conveyor binds them over HTTP. This supersedes ADR 0004's inclusion of those two services in this repository.
  3. The coding-* fleet and the w2v services are rewritten by the conveyor itself, not by hand-built absorption lanes. They are programme goals, not a queue of manual rewrites: T13 (tool platform) and T6 (development project) are what enable them, and the first proof is one service rewritten end to end by a conveyor job. No absorption issue is opened for them until that proof exists.
  4. The public tracker is the only tracker. Internal programme work stays in the public issue queue, sanitized: no hostname, address, private repository slug or private path in an issue body or comment. No private programme repository is created.

Context

ADR 0004 defined the tool estate and said every in-scope service joins this repository when it is rewritten in Rust. That stands for the services the conveyor calls today. It did not decide who performs a rewrite, and it put two services in scope that are shared with another product and are already Rust.

Measured 2026-11-13: strata_ingest and strata_rag are Rust and private, and both the agent platform and the conveyor use them. The coding fleet is public Python behind coding-gateway; the w2v fleet is private Python plus a TypeScript frontend.

What this supersedes

  • ADR 0004's in-scope list: strata_ingest and strata_rag remain tool-estate members but are not repository scope. Every other entry in 0004 stands.
  • ADR 0004's "how a service joins" procedure is unchanged for the hand-performed absorptions (#44–#46). It does not describe the conveyor-executed rewrites, which follow the T6 development project path and land in this repository when the conveyor produces them.

Consequences

  • After #44 the next two units are #45 and #46; the product spine waits.
  • The two strata services keep their own deploys, and their source stays private until a separate open-sourcing decision.
  • The coding and w2v rewrites appear in the programme as goals with named enabling slices, not as issues that can be claimed today.
  • Issue hygiene becomes part of the delivery protocol: checked at creation and reported by the sweep.
