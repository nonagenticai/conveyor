Project brief — T17 Tenant Asset Authoring (retrospective, 2026-11-27)

Outcome

T17 delivers tenant asset authoring: a tenant's short brief plus its documents become a reviewed catalogue of departments, jobs, procedures and policies that a person published, and no authored asset can publish itself or widen a tool grant. The outcome the record closes on is the acceptance manifest at a fresh snapshot plus the published evidence document, not the built status alone.

Boundary

In scope are the authoring lifecycle (intake, draft, review, publish-through-the-target), the two authoring tables, the state machine, the review gate, the published-dependency refusal and the Authoring screen. Out of scope are writing the target tables, the SOP/RDF/BPMN converter (T16), tools and bindings (T13), a governed_asset fork and a new template table except asset_template. The boundary is the inception brief's, restated so a later reader can see the exclusions are deliberate.

Reuse sources

T17 reuses the existing table and action surfaces (the target tables publish themselves), the governed_asset and document model (the published asset and its source), T3's approval and decision ladder (the review gate), T13's tools (request, never grant) and the workspace crates. It ships two authoring tables and five actions, not a second catalogue.

Inception units

The inception set is published: the project brief, the inception analysis (01a1ee42-312b-770c-bdae-36a1b077d2e6) and the architecture design (01a1ee42-3aca-7179-867c-000d986a865e), plus the evidence and closure-review documents. The delivery unit is #87, merged through PRs #632, #659 and #687; the Authoring screen and the E2E are part of the delivery.

Dependencies

T17 depends on T16 (live ingestion and the SOP human gate), T3 (the decision ladder) and P0 (the programme definition). All three are closed; the live procedure/policy ingestion remains T16's, and T17 proves the linked drafts and the refusals.

Acceptance

The acceptance is acceptance/programme/T17.toml with 15 checks, backed by the authoring lifecycle tests, the asset-review refusals, the output-standard checks and the real-app authoring E2E. The gate is the inception brief's: a brief plus documents draft the catalogue; a reviewed catalogue publishes with its decisions; no asset publishes itself; no asset widens a tool grant.

Capabilities

The built capability set is sixfold: intake (accept a brief and documents), drafting (organisation, job, procedure and policy assets with sources), review (a human gate refusing self-publication and non-human decisions), publication (through the target's own action with its decisions), the catalogue read (the Authoring screen) and the real-app E2E proof.

Plan

The plan is SPEC_V2 §5.7/§10.4–§10.5 and §12 T17, delivered through #87 and its follow-ups #629 and #655. This rework does not rebuild it: it supplies the lifecycle record the delivery did not leave behind, re-runs the acceptance at a fresh snapshot and keeps T17 active. The remaining work is a publish-time dependency check if the programme wants that guarantee, and T16's live ingestion.
