Ten requirements cover the skeleton. The first two make tenancy structural: eleven tables and forced row-level security, including the owner role. The next two make identity and entitlement fail closed: OIDC with a dev path that production refuses, and a licence row whose absence is a typed refusal. The last three make the surface observable and honest: SSE replay by Last-Event-ID, an append-only audit, and a served contract that matches its committed lock.
