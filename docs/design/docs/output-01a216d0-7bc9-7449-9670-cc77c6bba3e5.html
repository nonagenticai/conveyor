Project brief

Outcome

The conveyor ingests SOPs, RDF/OWL ontologies and BPMN/Visio/draw.io documents into governed procedure drafts that a person publishes, and a bound job executes the PDDL steps.

Boundary

Not in scope: the procedure catalogue UI (T18) and the process engine (S13).

Reuse sources

The action spine's governed assets and documents, the capability/job families, the process executor (S13), and the D53 decision (knowledge pack, no RDF store at runtime).

Inception units

The ingestion actions (procedures.ingest_*), the review/publish gates, and the re-ingest versioning path.

Dependencies

T2 (plan spine), T13 (governed assets), P0 (programme definition); S13 for execution.

Acceptance

SPEC_V2 §12 T16, proven by 25/25 named tests at lane head 46fc49e2; manifest acceptance/programme/T16.toml.

Capabilities

A team can turn its written procedures into executable, versioned procedures without hand-authoring the action space.

Plan

Delivered in tranche 4; the PI plan for tranche 4 carries the objectives and capacity.

Risks

The systemic-improvement loop (T5) and the operations path (T7) are the coupled slices; the human gate and the knowledge-pack decision are the mitigations.
