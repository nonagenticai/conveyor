Benefits management strategy — P0 (retrospective, 2026-11-26)

Measurement methods

The strategy published on 2026-11-18 at 08:18:00 (01a1e9e1-fd54-731e-945d-45d8e9f2d2a4) fixes the measurement method per benefit: the affected KPI, the baseline taken while the old practice is observable, the target, the method and the financial value where expressible. The method is recorded in the profile, and the comparison with the baseline is the evidence a sponsor uses. An LLM may draft narrative but not the numbers.

Review cycle

Measurement is reviewed at the tranche reviews, where the sponsoring group decides continue, pause or stop on the baselines, benefit status and risk register. A re-baseline goes through change control and never through the review itself, so the review cannot rewrite the evidence it judges. The tranche 0 review on 2026-11-17 and the tranche 1 authorisation on 2026-11-21 are the recorded instances of the cycle.

Roles and assessment

The BCM owns each benefit and assesses realisation; the SRO is accountable for the coherent capability and the benefits; the PMO lead and quality manager assure the governance baseline; the sponsoring group makes the tranche decision. Assessment happens per benefit at its transition, not at programme closure alone. What is measurable now is the role assignment in the strategy and the six profiles, not an assessment outcome.

Realization process

The process runs measure, monitor, plan the transition, communicate the change, assess readiness, enact, measure post-transition performance, retire the superseded practice, and continue measuring under the realisation plan. P0 defined the procedure and named who continues after closure. The P0 record contains no transition or post-transition measurement, so the procedure is defined and unexercised in the record, and no improvement is claimed.
