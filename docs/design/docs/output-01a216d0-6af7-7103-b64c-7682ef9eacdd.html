Goals and outcomes

The PM path's goal is an auditable request-to-closure loop; the outcome is that no gate, baseline or acceptance in the programme moves without a recorded, distinct decision.

Measures

  • Every gate carries a rule decision under a published delegation (measured by the approval ledger).
  • A baseline mutation after the frozen baseline cites an approved change (measured by the change rows).
  • Closure resolves against the baseline (measured by the closure gate's evidence).

Benefits and sub-benefits

The programme benefits served: B1 (auditable decisions) directly; B6 (the coverage the record gives) and B7 (through fewer rework loops) indirectly. The sub-benefits are the five accepted T3 deliverables.
