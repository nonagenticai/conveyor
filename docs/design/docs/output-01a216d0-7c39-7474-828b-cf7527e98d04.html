Project brief — T7 Operations (retrospective, 2026-11-27)

Outcome

T7 delivers the operations lifecycle: an incident card is opened, triaged, mitigated through an approved gated write (or a no-action record), postmortemed and resolved; every destructive ops write is refused before the cluster unless it carries an approval, a recorded dry run and two distinct human approvers; and restart, scale, rollback and flag-flip are callable.

Boundary

In scope are the five incident actions, the four gated write verbs, the k8s read family, the incident meta lifecycle and the /operations console. Out of scope are tools and bindings (T13), the agent tool surface (the ops writes are not agent-reachable) and a second ops service. The boundary is SPEC_V2 §6.5/§9.2 and §12 T7.

Reuse sources

T7 reuses the existing card and approval primitives (the incident card, the approval and approval_decision rows, the governed_asset postmortem, the process run for the kata), T2's job/event/audit rows and T4's registry and destructive effect. It ships no second ops service and no duplicate of T13's tools.

Inception units

The delivery unit is issue #78 (PR #438) for the incident lifecycle and the four write verbs, plus PR #434 for the /operations console. The executed evidence is T7-acceptance.md (31 tests, 2026-11-24); the two P1 findings v1-w1b-4 and v1-w3a-5 are fixed at ca155fada.

Dependencies

T7 depends on T2 (jobs/events/audit), T4 (registry/scopes), T13 (tools) and P0. All are closed; the live cluster proof is deploy-bound on the deployed image carrying ca155fada.

Acceptance

The acceptance is acceptance/programme/T7.toml with 17 checks, backed by the executed suite in T7-acceptance.md: triage, the approved mitigation and read-back, the postmortem and resolution, the refused unapproved write, the callable four verbs, the policy quorum, the systemic kata and the RLS/read family. The live cluster script is not claimed as run.

Capabilities

The built capability set is sixfold: incident intake, triage and runbooks, the four gated write verbs, mitigation (approved write or no action), the postmortem/kata loop and the /operations console. The §6.5 gate and the no-action mitigation are the two fixes that make the set conformant.

Plan

The plan is SPEC_V2 §6.5, §9.2 and §12 T7, delivered through #78 and #434. This rework does not rebuild it: it supplies the lifecycle record, closes the project on the executed suite and the two fixes, and carries the live cluster proof as a deploy-bound open item.
