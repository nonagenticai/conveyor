Safe Art — PLATFORM

ART charter

The ART is the programme's delivery rhythm: a PI per tranche, a refinement before it, a retro after it, and the demo credit on each accepted deliverable. Its authority is the programme owner's standing delegation and its scope is the programme's projects, not a tenant's product. The charter names the ART's outcomes so a review can judge them.

Solution intent

The ART intends a programme that delivers at quality with the least rework: tranche 0 green with its seven members evidenced, tranches 1–4 reworked in order, the benefit realization records written, and the public pack carrying the current record. The intent's constraints are the owner's directions and the estate's rules, which the deliverable's evidence cites.

Backlog with WSJF

The backlog is the programme's accepted deliverables, each carrying the four WSJF inputs the PI plan records. The platform's own deliverable scores business value 5, time criticality 5, risk reduction 3 and job size 6; the ordering is the programme's, and a deliverable without its four inputs is not on the backlog.

Shared configuration

The programme's shared configuration carries the cadence, the job-family pins, the default approval policy and the knowledge pins its projects inherit. It is a real row, written through programmes.update; an empty object is absence, not a configuration, and the standard treats it that way.
