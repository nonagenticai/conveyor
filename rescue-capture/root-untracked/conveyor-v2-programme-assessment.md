# Conveyor V2 programme execution assessment

**Conveyor has built most of the V2 platform and used parts of TOGAF and MSP to govern its own buildout. The strongest evidence is P0’s programme definition and first tranche decision, plus T9’s adoption of a tailored ADM profile and its Phase G compliance review. The evidence stops short of programme-wide architecture governance, progressive tranche approval, and measured benefits.** Those gaps matter because the specification makes all three part of the programme’s own acceptance. [SPEC V2, §§8 and 12](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/SPEC_V2.html)

I compared GitHub `main` at `374ba9f` with the public programme snapshot generated on **20 November 2026 at 11:50 UTC**, which reports deployed revision `a33e656`. I checked the published outputs, selected issues and PRs, and the gate code. I ran the repository’s coverage, document-index and native parity-map checks. Live verification was limited to public endpoints and published evidence. [Repository revision](https://github.com/nonagenticai/conveyor/commit/374ba9f3f2ad5195c02db510458dce14d04eb17c) · [Snapshot manifest](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/data/manifest.json)

## How TOGAF was followed

- **Preliminary phase was applied.** P0 published architecture principles, a tailored ADM profile, an artefact standard, and maturity and skills assessments. The public register shows the adopted profile, three approved Preliminary artefacts, two trace links, and an Architecture Board record. The board record itself still carries `draft` status. The adopted profile lists Preliminary and A–H, with mandatory artefact sets defined through D. [P0 plan](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/plans/P0-programme-definition.html) · [Live architecture register](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/data/architecture.json)
- **Phase A-style commissioning and Phase G governance were exercised on T9.** T9 has a live Statement of Architecture Work and a published Phase G review that judged delivery **compliant** against that contract. The review records that A–F phase progression was demonstrated in tests and that no live architecture project had exercised phase advance. The public register shows one live contract, for T9. The issue to put every delivery project under an approved contract is still open. [T9 compliance review](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/output-01a1f360-3420-73f4-8f42-b636a32c29c1.html) · [Programme-wide contract issue](https://github.com/nonagenticai/conveyor/issues/395)

My assessment is **a tailored ADM capability was established and exercised on one delivery**. The public evidence for iterating through the ADM and governing the wider buildout is still limited. The distinction matters because the ADM covers development of the architecture, migration planning, and implementation governance across the work it governs. [The Open Group on ADM phases](https://help.opengroup.org/hc/en-us/articles/32115987894930-How-the-ArchiMate-Language-and-the-TOGAF-Standard-Complement-Each-Other) · [The Open Group on applying and tailoring the ADM](https://help.opengroup.org/hc/en-us/articles/32127544219026-Competency-to-Role-Mapping-TOGAF-Enterprise-Architecture-Practitioner)

## How MSP was followed

- **Programme definition and the first sponsor decision are evidenced.** P0 is closed and all **21 required output categories** are represented in the public index. They include the mandate and vision, blueprint, business case, projects dossier, benefits map and profiles, stakeholder profiles, tranche plan, governance, and realization plan. The programme owner’s recorded tranche 0 decision was `continue`, explicitly authorising tranche 1. The business case sets out the rationale and cost and risk posture. I found no quantified investment appraisal or cash-flow schedule in the published case. [P0 plan](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/plans/P0-programme-definition.html) · [Business case](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/output-01a1e006-e2ba-7372-87c7-61dbb38d2d6e.html) · [Tranche 0 decision](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/output-01a1eb18-6752-72ff-9c28-cd4a94217de7.html)
- **Progressive tranche control is only partly evidenced.** The snapshot contains 14 tranches and one review. It still shows tranches **1–13 as planned**, including tranche 1 despite the published authorisation. The programme register also shows T1 closed before the tranche review was entered, and several tranche 4 projects already closed. GitHub construction, Conveyor runs, and project closure have distinct records. Taken together, the public records do not demonstrate that the stated tranche sequence governed delivery throughout. The snapshot also shows zero structured programme baselines and register items, while the first review records the accepted definition and risks in its evidence. [Live MSP register](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/data/msp.json) · [Project register](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/data/programme.json) · [Tranche gate implementation](https://github.com/nonagenticai/conveyor/blob/374ba9f/crates/actions/src/programme.rs)
- **Benefits management is defined and realization is unproven.** Six profiles and six map links exist. The first review records the Business Change Manager as unstaffed. T10’s own closure review also identifies a concrete gap: the transition path requires a measured baseline, while the generic `benefits.update` path can set `realized` without that transition. I confirmed that path in the reviewed revision. For your original thesis, the six published profiles also lack a comparative target for token spend per accepted deliverable. The programme evidence therefore cannot yet establish that Conveyor reduces token spend for equivalent work. [Realization plan](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/output-01a1e9e2-275e-754f-836b-8738df8fdd8f.html) · [T10 closure review](https://conveyor.nonagentic.ai/design/docs/output-01a1f2ca-31ea-7341-a725-4c4492c7d3f7.html) · [Benefit update code](https://github.com/nonagenticai/conveyor/blob/374ba9f/crates/actions/src/spine/mod.rs#L2142)
