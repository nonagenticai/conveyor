licenses — live served OpenAPI vs the captured lock
====================================================

Measured 2026-11-14, from the master host, against the LIVE service:

  # the service address is an in-cluster name, redacted for the public tree
  ssh -i <deploy key> <login>@<master> \
    "curl -sk --max-time 15 https://<licenses-service>/openapi.json" > live.json
  jq -S . live.json > live.sorted.json
  jq -S . apps/licenses/contract/openapi.lock.json > lock.sorted.json
  sha256sum live.sorted.json lock.sorted.json
